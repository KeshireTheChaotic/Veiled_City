# City Locations
**Visibility: PLAYER SAFE**

These locations are public, broadly rumored in the occult community, or appropriate for a player to know without learning campaign secrets. Additional locations should be added to the player state only after discovery.

## The Lantern House
A converted brick firehouse used as a local Lantern office, clinic, archive, and very imperfect safehouse.

## Saint Arden Emergency
A busy teaching hospital where Dr. Victor Shaw works nights.

## Westway Mall
A mostly abandoned suburban mall with redevelopment signs that never quite lead to redevelopment.

## The Blue Hour
A stylish nightclub favored by Pale Court clients and ordinary professionals who think they are being adventurous.

## The Rook Building
Aster Vale Risk’s glass-and-steel regional headquarters.

## Kestrel Data Annex
A municipal data center and traffic-control facility.

## Riverside Marker 14
An old stone marker beside a levee trail. Local folklore gives it more significance than its plaque does.

## Mercy House
Father Ortiz’s sanctuary and mutual-aid center.

## Harridan & Patel
A small law office handling property, contracts, and several clients who prefer not to be photographed.

## The Municipal Archive
A public archive beneath the old courthouse.

## Crown & Ash Townhouse
A historic property associated in occult rumor with formal Pale Court meetings.

## Switchyard 19
Rail, fiber, power, and drainage infrastructure converge in one fenced industrial corridor.

## Linden Community Gardens
A thriving garden network on land repeatedly targeted for development.

## The Meridian Hotel
An aging downtown hotel with more conference rooms than guests.

## Foxglove Storage
A self-storage facility near the highway with a reputation for unusual late-night traffic.

## Old Bell Telephone Exchange
A decommissioned switching building whose copper, conduits, and sealed rooms attract urban explorers.

## North River Pump Station
Mundane flood-control infrastructure on the river.

## The Museum Annex
A climate-controlled storage building for artifacts not currently on display.

## County Morgue B
An older morgue wing still used for overflow, evidence holds, and night work.

## Red Line Memorial
An informal cluster of flowers, transit cards, and handwritten names at a station wall.

> **Discovery rule:** Secret refuges, impossible places, hidden facilities, and metaplot locations belong in `GM_PRIVATE/` until the PC discovers them. The AI GM then adds only the newly known player-facing description to `STATE/CAMPAIGN_STATE.json`.
