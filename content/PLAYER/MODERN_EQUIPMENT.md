# Modern & Occult Equipment
**Visibility: PLAYER SAFE • PLAYTEST HOMEBREW**

These options use Daggerheart weapon/armor abstraction. Ammunition, magazines, fuel, and batteries are narrative unless scarcity becomes materially important.

## Consumables and field gear
- **Black Salt Ampoule:** break it to reveal invisible footprints or crossings through a Very Close area for a few minutes.
- **Silver Nitrate Spray:** one-use narrative silver treatment for a small object or Melee weapon for a scene.
- **Cold-Iron Nails:** establish a crude Very Close anti-fae boundary during a moment of preparation; it inconveniences rather than absolutely bars powerful entities.
- **Emergency Ward Strip:** apply to a doorway; the first hostile supernatural creature crossing it becomes Vulnerable until its next spotlight.
- **Ghost Battery:** power one supernatural or Signal device for a scene without ordinary electricity.
- **Burner Phone:** clean mundane handset with no established connection to the PC until used.
- **Red Thread Kit:** gain advantage on one roll to construct or identify a sympathetic link during a ritual.
- **Mourner's Candle:** while lit, nearby ghosts can manifest visibly if they choose.
- **Veil Chalk:** gives advantage on one roll to create a temporary threshold, circle, or occult boundary.
- **Glass Market Scrip:** an abstract favor-token accepted by some Market vendors; value varies by stall and night.
- **Mnemonic Gum:** chew during a rest to perfectly preserve the next short conversation you hear until your next long rest.
- **Static Coin:** flip it near hidden electronics; it lands edge-up when an active concealed transmitter is within Very Close range.

## Mundane kit
If your background reasonably includes an ordinary smartphone, laptop, flashlight, basic tools, clothing, wallet, charger, vehicle access, medical supplies, or professional identification, the GM should not make those objects rare treasure. Track them only when their loss or scarcity matters.
