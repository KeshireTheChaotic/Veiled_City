# GM ORACLE
**Visibility: ENGINE - player-readable procedure; individual hidden rolls may remain GM-private.**

Use when uncertainty should be impartial rather than authored.

## Yes/No Oracle - d12
1: No, and...
2-4: No.
5: No, but...
6-7: Uncertain / mixed; introduce a clue or choice.
8: Yes, but...
9-11: Yes.
12: Yes, and...

If overwhelmingly likely, shift result two steps toward Yes.
If likely, shift one step toward Yes.
If unlikely, shift one step toward No.
If overwhelmingly unlikely, shift two steps toward No.

## Scene Twist - d12
1 Betrayal or divided loyalty
2 An innocent becomes involved
3 Evidence contradicts the obvious theory
4 A mundane authority arrives
5 A supernatural faction intervenes
6 The environment becomes dangerous
7 Time pressure escalates
8 Something valuable is offered at a cost
9 The threat changes tactics
10 An old consequence returns
11 A hidden relationship is exposed
12 The apparent problem is only a symptom

## Clue vector - d8
1 Physical trace
2 Witness behavior
3 Digital record
4 Occult resonance
5 Financial/legal trail
6 Historical connection
7 Relationship/social clue
8 Contradiction or absence
