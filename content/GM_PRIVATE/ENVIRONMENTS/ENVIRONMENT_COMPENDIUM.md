# Veiled City Environment Compendium
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — SPOILERS**

## Tier 1

### Occult Crime Scene
**Tier 1 Exploration**
A mundane incident whose physical evidence does not agree with the official explanation.
**Impulses:** Preserve evidence, attract authorities, reveal contradictions
**Difficulty:** 11
**Potential Adversaries:** Aster Vale Contractor, Veil-Burned Stalker, Memory Leech

**Features**
- **Contaminated Scene — Passive:** The first careless supernatural effect used here raises Veil Exposure by 1 if mundane investigators are present.
- **Three Vectors — Action:** Offer three clue vectors from physical, digital, witness, occult, financial, historical, relationship, or contradiction evidence. The PC chooses one to pursue; on Fear, another interested party moves on a different vector.
- **Sirens Outside — Action:** Spend a Fear to bring mundane authority, media, property management, or emergency response into the scene at an inconvenient moment.

### After-Hours Emergency Department
**Tier 1 Social**
Bright fluorescent medicine, exhausted staff, frightened families, and one patient whose chart makes no sense.
**Impulses:** Triage, demand explanations, protect patients
**Difficulty:** 11
**Potential Adversaries:** Memory Leech, Pale Court Thrall, Static Wraith

**Features**
- **HIPAA and Procedure — Passive:** Routine access to patient information requires appropriate credentials, consent, or a credible workaround.
- **Code Blue — Action:** A mundane medical emergency competes for staff attention. The PC can help, exploit the distraction, or let it complicate the supernatural problem.
- **Something in Imaging — Action:** Reveal an impossible detail in a scan, monitor, or lab result that provides a clue but creates a record.

### Abandoned Mall Glass Market
**Tier 1 Social**
A dead retail complex temporarily repopulated by impossible vendors and rules of neutral commerce.
**Impulses:** Tempt, trade, enforce neutrality
**Difficulty:** 12
**Potential Adversaries:** Cicada Runner, Glass Market Knife, Hexbound Familiar

**Features**
- **No Violence in the Aisles — Passive:** The first party to knowingly break Market neutrality immediately loses standing and attracts enforcement.
- **Everything Has a Price — Action:** Present a useful item or answer at a cost tailored to the PC’s current problem. State the price before acceptance.
- **Closing Time Moves — Action:** Spend a Fear; the Market begins relocating. The PC has only one more meaningful exchange before exits lead somewhere else.

### Storm Drain Shrine
**Tier 1 Exploration**
Graffiti, runoff, offerings, candle wax, and a local spirit that knows every footstep above.
**Impulses:** Echo, carry offerings, flood
**Difficulty:** 11
**Potential Adversaries:** River Hound, Hexbound Familiar

**Features**
- **Local Memory — Passive:** Respectful offerings grant advantage on one question about recent movement in the area.
- **Flash Flood — Action:** All targets in a low channel make an Agility Reaction Roll (11) or take 1d8+2 physical damage and move to Very Close range downstream.
- **Wrong Offering — Reaction:** When someone desecrates or carelessly removes an offering, the environment becomes hostile until restitution is made.

### Apartment Veil Breach
**Tier 1 Event**
A supernatural incident is unfolding inside an ordinary occupied building with too many neighbors and cameras.
**Impulses:** Escalate witnesses, trap people in domestic spaces, leave evidence
**Difficulty:** 11
**Potential Adversaries:** Static Wraith, Veil-Burned Stalker, Morrowline Echo

**Features**
- **Thin Walls — Passive:** Loud or spectacular actions risk adding witnesses.
- **Door Chain — Action:** A frightened resident blocks the most direct path and demands a believable explanation.
- **Upload in Progress — Action:** Spend a Fear to start a 4-step countdown before clear footage leaves the building; a successful relevant action can tick it back or redirect it.
## Tier 2

### Dead Platform
**Tier 2 Traversal**
A subway platform that exists between scheduled stops and does not appear on official maps.
**Impulses:** Separate travelers, change destinations, arrive at the wrong time
**Difficulty:** 14
**Potential Adversaries:** Traffic Saint, Wraith Pack, Glass Market Knife

**Features**
- **Last Train Rules — Passive:** Every departure requires a destination, a fare, and a stated reason. Lying to the platform increases Difficulty by 2.
- **Express Through Memory — Action:** The PC makes a Knowledge Reaction Roll (14); on a failure, the next train opens onto a location associated with a painful memory rather than the intended stop.
- **Third Rail Spirit — Action:** Spend a Fear to manifest a hostile current across a route, dealing 2d8+3 magic damage on a failed Agility Reaction Roll.

### Aster Vale Predictive Lab
**Tier 2 Exploration**
Corporate glass, biometric access, spotless server rooms, and occult models hidden inside legitimate analytics.
**Impulses:** Record, classify, lock down
**Difficulty:** 14
**Potential Adversaries:** Aster Vale Contractor, Occult Analyst, Forecast Engine

**Features**
- **Everything Logs — Passive:** Failed intrusion actions create a trace unless the PC spends Hope or has a credible way to suppress it.
- **Model Room — Action:** Reveal a forecast that is frighteningly accurate but incomplete. The PC may take a Hope by accepting that one listed risk is real and immediately relevant.
- **Containment Shutters — Action:** Spend a Fear to divide the area with security barriers; escaping or reuniting routes requires a Difficulty 14 action.

### Haunted Nightclub
**Tier 2 Social**
Music, intoxication, glamour, predation, and a dance floor built over an older tragedy.
**Impulses:** Overstimulate, seduce, conceal predation
**Difficulty:** 14
**Potential Adversaries:** Pale Court Knight, Skin-Thief, Wraith Pack

**Features**
- **Too Loud to Think — Passive:** Delicate auditory or verbal investigation has disadvantage unless the PC changes the environment.
- **VIP Threshold — Passive:** Entry to the private floor requires invitation, status, leverage, or deception.
- **Everybody Saw Something Different — Action:** After a supernatural incident, give three mutually inconsistent witness accounts; at least one detail from each is true.

### Pale Court Townhouse
**Tier 2 Social**
A beautifully restored property where hospitality is weaponized through etiquette and ownership.
**Impulses:** Rank, invite, obligate
**Difficulty:** 15
**Potential Adversaries:** Pale Court Fixer, Pale Court Knight

**Features**
- **Guest Right — Passive:** A guest who accepts formal hospitality gains protection from unprovoked violence by the host but is expected to obey explicitly stated house rules.
- **Private Room — Action:** An NPC offers a confidential conversation that grants leverage but separates the PC from allies or exits.
- **Ownership Bites — Reaction:** When a guest deliberately violates a known house rule, the environment may inflict 2d6+3 magic damage or make them Vulnerable until they leave.

### Flooded River Threshold
**Tier 2 Traversal**
A riverfront access road, levee, or bridge where physical water and the Green overlap.
**Impulses:** Pull under, demand respect, erase boundaries
**Difficulty:** 14
**Potential Adversaries:** River Hound, Thorn-Hound of the Green

**Features**
- **Current with Intent — Action:** A target makes a Strength Reaction Roll (14); on a failure take 2d8+3 physical damage and move toward deep water.
- **Old Marker Stone — Passive:** Discovering and honoring the original boundary reduces the environment Difficulty by 2.
- **City vs River — Action:** Spend a Fear to make infrastructure fail—lights, pumps, gates, traffic control—creating a second simultaneous problem.
## Tier 3

### Glass Market Grand Concourse
**Tier 3 Social**
A major Market convergence where factions trade artifacts, identities, memories, and political outcomes.
**Impulses:** Auction, tempt, expose debt
**Difficulty:** 17
**Potential Adversaries:** Debt-Reaper, Pale Court Elder, Cicada Runner

**Features**
- **Neutrality Has Teeth — Passive:** Open violence immediately draws Tier 3 enforcement unless a formal duel or debt collection permits it.
- **Auction of Consequences — Action:** Put a campaign-relevant relic, secret, or person’s contract up for sale. Bids can include non-monetary costs.
- **Everyone Is Watching — Reaction:** When the PC makes a public threat, promise, or bargain, record it as witnessed by at least one faction.

### Archive Beneath the Courthouse
**Tier 3 Exploration**
A sealed municipal archive containing legal records older than the city’s official memory.
**Impulses:** Preserve precedent, expose ownership, punish unauthorized access
**Difficulty:** 17
**Potential Adversaries:** Veil Anchor Sentinel, Name-Eater

**Features**
- **Chain of Title — Passive:** Ownership, threshold, and pact magic used here can often be traced through mundane property records.
- **Sealed Docket — Action:** Reveal a document that changes the legal or supernatural interpretation of a current conflict. Accessing it requires sacrificing time, secrecy, or a favor.
- **Records Reject You — Action:** Spend a Fear; the PC makes a Presence Reaction Roll (17). On a failure, their name temporarily disappears from one mundane database or document set until corrected.

### District Blackout
**Tier 3 Event**
A large urban area loses power while supernatural systems become much louder.
**Impulses:** Isolate, reroute, expose dependence
**Difficulty:** 17
**Potential Adversaries:** Choir Junction Intelligence, Blacksite Null Team, Catastrophic Echo

**Features**
- **No Ordinary Signal — Passive:** Cellular and normal network access is unreliable; alternate infrastructure or Signal magic is required.
- **Emergency Cascade — Action:** Choose hospital, traffic, fire, elevator, refrigeration, or public safety. A real mundane emergency develops and competes for attention.
- **Choir Volume — Action:** Spend a Fear to make every powered backup system in Close range speak with the same voice. The PC marks a Stress unless they can ground themselves.

### Veil Anchor Chamber
**Tier 3 Exploration**
An impossible ritual machine hidden inside ordinary civic architecture.
**Impulses:** Correct anomalies, preserve secrecy, demand continuity
**Difficulty:** 18
**Potential Adversaries:** Veil Anchor Sentinel, Morrowline Matured

**Features**
- **Consensus Engine — Passive:** Obvious supernatural actions inside may alter Veil Exposure in unexpected directions; the GM should state the risk before a voluntary action.
- **Maintenance Memory — Action:** The chamber shows a historical scene associated with the Anchor’s creation, revealing one true piece of campaign metaplot.
- **Automatic Correction — Action:** Spend a Fear to rewrite one mundane environmental detail in the chamber and force the PC to adapt.
## Tier 4

### Rupture District
**Tier 4 Traversal**
A neighborhood where the Veil has failed and incompatible supernatural truths overlap the modern city.
**Impulses:** Manifest, contradict, attract power
**Difficulty:** 20
**Potential Adversaries:** Veil Engine Custodian, Citywide Choir, Sovereign of the Green

**Features**
- **No Consensus — Passive:** Each major scene may obey a different supernatural logic. State the current rule clearly when the PC can perceive it.
- **Reality Collision — Action:** A target makes an Instinct Reaction Roll (20). On a failure, take 4d8+8 magic damage and become Vulnerable as two incompatible versions of the space overlap.
- **Mass Witness — Reaction:** Spectacular actions automatically affect Veil Exposure; there is no ordinary crowd-level concealment left.

### Networked Dream
**Tier 4 Exploration**
A shared dream-space assembled from millions of devices, memories, feeds, and machine-spirit associations.
**Impulses:** Associate, remix identity, erase distance
**Difficulty:** 20
**Potential Adversaries:** Citywide Choir, Hunger Behind Glass

**Features**
- **Metaphor Is Physics — Passive:** Clever symbolic actions can substitute for literal tools if the player explains the connection.
- **Algorithmic Undertow — Action:** The PC makes a Knowledge Reaction Roll (20). On a failure, lose 1 Hope and become trapped in a personalized feed until they reject or disrupt its premise.
- **Viral Door — Action:** Spend a Fear to introduce a memory, rumor, advertisement, or fear from earlier in the campaign as a physical location or adversarial obstacle.
