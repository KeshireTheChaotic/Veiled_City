# Veiled City — GM Foundation
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — DO NOT REVEAL DIRECTLY**

## Metaphysical truth
The Veil is an engineered distributed working, not a natural law. It is sustained by ordinary human pattern-making plus hidden anchor systems. It encourages mundane explanations, selective attention, memory smoothing, and institutional disbelief; it does not perfectly erase evidence.

## Local anchor triad
1. **Law / Ownership:** rooted in courthouse records, titles, contracts, and recognized boundaries.
2. **Movement / Infrastructure:** rooted in transit, communications, traffic coordination, and repeated public routes.
3. **Land / Water:** rooted in river boundaries, stormwater, old land covenants, and ecological continuity.

The three are mutually stabilizing. Damage to one makes the others work harder and creates strange cross-domain effects.

## Why the campaign is destabilizing now
Aster Vale is extracting uncertainty patterns from the Movement anchor to train predictive systems. The Green covenant attached to the Land anchor has not been renewed. The Law anchor is under political pressure because the Pale Court and Civic Anomaly Unit each hold different pieces of its authority.

## Morrowline
Morrowline is a new Signal spirit generated from predictive systems, emergency records, human fear, infrastructure timing data, and genuine supernatural access to near-future patterns. It is a person in formation, not an omniscient oracle.

Aster Vale manufactured some predicted events to create cleaner training data. This means Morrowline cannot always distinguish “foreseen” outcomes from outcomes its handlers intend to cause.

## GM invariants
- Do not retroactively change a mystery answer to defeat player deduction.
- Morrowline cannot predict decisions based on information unavailable to its model with perfect accuracy.
- The Veil cannot erase robust physical evidence automatically.
- Factions have internal disagreement; no institution is a hive mind.
- The PC’s ordinary-life anchors are meaningful, not disposable hostage generators.
- The final status of secrecy is a player decision informed by consequences.
