# Urban Chase Toolkit
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY**

Use this with `PLAYER/VEHICLES.md` and the normal Daggerheart action economy. Do not turn every chase into repeated Agility rolls.

## Chase pressure
Track an internal three-state position: **Hunter Advantage / Contested / Quarry Advantage**. Start Contested unless fiction says otherwise. A strong success can shift one step; a critical success can create an immediate escape/intercept opportunity. A Fear result can shift against the PC or introduce a complication instead.

Do not reveal the track as a number unless the player requests a more tactical presentation.

## d12 urban chase complications
1. Pedestrians enter the route.
2. Emergency vehicles block the obvious path.
3. Construction collapses two lanes into one.
4. A traffic camera has a perfect angle on the supernatural event.
5. The pursuer creates a mundane accident as cover.
6. A Veil distortion makes navigation instructions contradictory.
7. A spirit claims the next bridge, tunnel, or intersection as territory.
8. A faction contact is unexpectedly nearby and may become involved.
9. The route crosses a crowded public event.
10. Weather turns visibility or traction dangerous.
11. A vehicle becomes Impaired at the worst moment.
12. The shortest route passes through a location relevant to an unresolved case.

## d8 supernatural pursuit moves
1. Appear in reflections before physically arriving.
2. Jump between active cameras or network nodes.
3. Mark the vehicle with a sympathetic trace.
4. Influence traffic signals or navigation systems.
5. Send a lesser creature ahead to close the route.
6. Offer the PC a bargain over the phone or radio.
7. Temporarily remove a street or exit from ordinary notice.
8. Force the chase across a threshold where different rules apply.

## Chase fairness
A failed chase should usually produce capture, lost ground, vehicle damage, exposure, debt, separation, or a forced confrontation—not arbitrary instant death. Apply severe physical consequences only when the fiction and prior stakes clearly support them.
