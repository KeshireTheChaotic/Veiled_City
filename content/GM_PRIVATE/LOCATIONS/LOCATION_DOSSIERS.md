# Location Dossiers
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — SPOILERS**

## The Lantern House
**Player-facing:** A converted brick firehouse used as a local Lantern office, clinic, archive, and very imperfect safehouse.

**Hidden layer:** The basement contains a sealed room from the prior cell’s hospital-haunting case.

## Saint Arden Emergency
**Player-facing:** A busy teaching hospital where Dr. Victor Shaw works nights.

**Hidden layer:** The oldest elevator occasionally opens onto a nonphysical “service floor” after deaths with unresolved supernatural causes.

## Westway Mall
**Player-facing:** A mostly abandoned suburban mall with redevelopment signs that never quite lead to redevelopment.

**Hidden layer:** One of the Glass Market’s most reliable local manifestations appears here after midnight.

## Platform Zero
**Player-facing:** A transit platform absent from official maps.

**Hidden layer:** It is a liminal junction linking several supernatural transit routes and one Veil-anchor maintenance path.

## The Blue Hour
**Player-facing:** A stylish nightclub favored by Pale Court clients and ordinary professionals who think they are being adventurous.

**Hidden layer:** The VIP threshold is bound to Court hospitality law.

## The Rook Building
**Player-facing:** Aster Vale Risk’s glass-and-steel regional headquarters.

**Hidden layer:** Three basement levels are missing from public plans; one houses the Forecast Lab.

## Kestrel Data Annex
**Player-facing:** A municipal data center and traffic-control facility.

**Hidden layer:** Mina hid part of Morrowline’s refusal-training set in redundant traffic archives.

## Riverside Marker 14
**Player-facing:** An old stone marker beside a levee trail.

**Hidden layer:** It records the original land covenant in a symbolic legal language Mother Juniper can interpret.

## Mercy House
**Player-facing:** Father Ortiz’s sanctuary and mutual-aid center.

**Hidden layer:** Its basement threshold becomes unusually stable during Veil disturbances.

## Harridan & Patel
**Player-facing:** A small law office handling property, contracts, and several clients who prefer not to be photographed.

**Hidden layer:** Samir keeps a physically handwritten index of supernatural contract precedents off-site.

## The Municipal Archive
**Player-facing:** A public archive beneath the old courthouse.

**Hidden layer:** A restricted sublevel contains the chain-of-title documents for the local Veil anchors.

## Crown & Ash Townhouse
**Player-facing:** Historic property used by the Pale Court for formal meetings.

**Hidden layer:** Regent Celene’s covenant fragment can temporarily disable one anchor safeguard from here.

## Switchyard 19
**Player-facing:** Rail, fiber, power, and drainage infrastructure converge in one fenced industrial corridor.

**Hidden layer:** The Choir treats the junction as a sacred crossroads.

## Linden Community Gardens
**Player-facing:** A thriving garden network on land repeatedly targeted for development.

**Hidden layer:** Mother Juniper can open a Green path from the oldest tool shed.

## The Meridian Hotel
**Player-facing:** An aging downtown hotel with more conference rooms than guests.

**Hidden layer:** The Glass Market sometimes occupies an entire impossible mezzanine during major auctions.

## Foxglove Storage
**Player-facing:** Self-storage facility near the highway.

**Hidden layer:** Lantern evidence lockers occupy three units under false company names.

## Old Bell Telephone Exchange
**Player-facing:** A decommissioned switching building.

**Hidden layer:** Switchboard’s seven component spirits can be addressed separately here.

## Aster Vale Safe Apartment
**Player-facing:** A furnished corporate lease in an ordinary residential tower.

**Hidden layer:** Used to isolate defectors, witnesses, or consultants under “protective relocation.”

## North River Pump Station
**Player-facing:** Mundane flood-control infrastructure on the river.

**Hidden layer:** One of three local anchor sites is physically threaded through the pump station’s oldest foundation.

## The Museum Annex
**Player-facing:** A climate-controlled storage building for artifacts not currently on display.

**Hidden layer:** The Civic Anomaly Unit’s pre-Veil artifact was quietly moved here under a false accession number.

## County Morgue B
**Player-facing:** Older morgue wing where Owen works nights.

**Hidden layer:** The refrigeration grid overlaps a grave-resonance current and attracts unusually coherent ghosts.

## Red Line Memorial
**Player-facing:** An informal cluster of flowers, transit cards, and names at a station wall.

**Hidden layer:** The “Saint of the Red Line” can manifest through the memorial during imminent transit deaths.

## House 404
**Player-facing:** A detached house that appears on navigation systems only under specific conditions.

**Hidden layer:** Its legal address was magically stolen; the house exists in the gap created by missing ownership records.

## The Anchor Chamber
**Player-facing:** A location the PC should not know at campaign start.

**Hidden layer:** The local Veil’s principal civic anchor is an impossible ritual structure layered through courthouse, transit, and river infrastructure rather than one room.
