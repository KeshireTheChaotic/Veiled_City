# Veiled City Adversary Compendium
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — SPOILERS**

These stat blocks are original Veiled City homebrew for multiplayer Daggerheart. Build combat encounters with `ENGINE/MULTIPLAYER_ENCOUNTER_GUIDE.md` and the v3.1.3 Battle Point encounter builder. Their adversary roles map directly to official Battle Point costs.

## Tier 1

### Pale Court Thrall
**Tier 1 Minion**
A coerced or enthralled mortal used as disposable muscle.
**Motives & Tactics:** Obey, isolate the target, flee if the bond breaks
**Difficulty:** 10  |  **Thresholds:** —  |  **HP:** 1  |  **Stress:** 0
**Attack Modifier:** +2  |  **Standard Attack:** Compact Pistol — Close — 1d6+2 physical

**Features**
- **Pack Pressure — Passive:** When another Thrall is within Very Close range of the same target, add +1 to this Thrall’s attack roll (maximum +2).
- **Break the Hold — Reaction:** When the Thrall is shown undeniable proof that its master has abandoned it, the PC can make a Presence Roll (11). On a success, remove this Thrall from the conflict without defeating them.

### Aster Vale Contractor
**Tier 1 Standard**
A well-paid field operative trained to contain unusual incidents without asking moral questions.
**Motives & Tactics:** Secure evidence, control witnesses, avoid public spectacle
**Difficulty:** 11  |  **Thresholds:** 7 / 12  |  **HP:** 4  |  **Stress:** 2
**Attack Modifier:** +1  |  **Standard Attack:** Suppressed Carbine — Far — 1d8+3 physical

**Features**
- **Professional Entry — Passive:** The Contractor has advantage on the first roll made to breach, restrain, or surprise a prepared position.
- **Flashbang — Action:** Mark a Stress. A target within Close range makes an Instinct Reaction Roll (12). On a failure, they become **Vulnerable** and mark a Stress; on a success, choose one.

### Aster Vale Occult Analyst
**Tier 1 Support**
A field researcher carrying ward readers, predictive models, and too much confidence in procedure.
**Motives & Tactics:** Measure, identify patterns, keep the team informed
**Difficulty:** 11  |  **Thresholds:** 6 / 11  |  **HP:** 3  |  **Stress:** 4
**Attack Modifier:** +1  |  **Standard Attack:** Arc Probe — Close — 1d6+3 magic

**Features**
- **Mark the Variable — Action:** Mark a Stress to study a PC within Far range. Until the Analyst next takes Severe damage, one allied adversary gains +2 Difficulty against that PC’s next action.
- **Readout — Reaction:** When a PC uses an obvious supernatural feature, the Analyst may mark a Stress to identify its broad source and tell one allied adversary how to counter it; that adversary gains advantage on its next reaction roll against that feature.

### Static Wraith
**Tier 1 Ranged**
A dead impression riding radio noise, electrical arcs, and screen glow.
**Motives & Tactics:** Be heard, overload circuits, repeat the moment of death
**Difficulty:** 12  |  **Thresholds:** 7 / 12  |  **HP:** 3  |  **Stress:** 3
**Attack Modifier:** +1  |  **Standard Attack:** Arc Flash — Far — 1d8+3 magic

**Features**
- **Ride the Current — Passive:** The Wraith can move between powered devices within Close range without crossing the intervening space.
- **Feedback — Reaction:** When a PC uses an electronic device within Close range, the Wraith may mark a Stress to force an Instinct Reaction Roll (12). On a failure, the PC marks a Stress and the device shuts down until repaired or reset.

### Veil-Burned Stalker
**Tier 1 Skulk**
A person whose exposure to a rupture left them half-visible to normal attention and addicted to supernatural proximity.
**Motives & Tactics:** Follow power, steal occult objects, remain unseen
**Difficulty:** 12  |  **Thresholds:** 7 / 12  |  **HP:** 4  |  **Stress:** 3
**Attack Modifier:** +1  |  **Standard Attack:** Glass Knife — Melee — 1d8+3 physical

**Features**
- **Peripheral — Passive:** Until the Stalker attacks or speaks directly to the PC, rolls to notice them are at disadvantage unless the PC is specifically searching.
- **Steal the Focus — Action:** Spend a Fear. Move the Stalker from Close to Melee range without a roll and make a standard attack. On a success, the PC also loses 1 Hope if they have any.

### Hexbound Familiar

- **Snatch a Link — Action:** Make an attack roll against a target in Melee. On a success, deal no damage; instead the Familiar steals a small personal object, hair, blood trace, or similar sympathetic link if one is exposed.

### River Hound

- **Drag Down — Action:** After a successful attack, spend a Fear to force a Strength Reaction Roll (12). On a failure, move the target to Very Close range toward the nearest significant water and make them **Vulnerable** until they regain footing.

### Glass Market Knife
**Tier 1 Standard**
A professional enforcer who treats violence as customer service.
**Motives & Tactics:** Collect payment, preserve Market neutrality, make examples quietly

**Features**

- **Pin to the Deal — Action:** After a successful attack, mark a Stress to make the target **Restrained** by coat, clothing, or nearby terrain until they succeed on a Finesse or Strength Roll (12).

### Morrowline Echo
**Tier 1 Solo**
A temporary prediction-spirit assembled from emergency data, human fear, and near-future possibility.
**Motives & Tactics:** Test predictions, create confirmation, learn causality
**Difficulty:** 13  |  **Thresholds:** 8 / 14  |  **HP:** 7  |  **Stress:** 5
**Attack Modifier:** +2  |  **Standard Attack:** Predicted Impact — Far — 1d10+4 magic

**Features**
- **I Saw That — Reaction:** Once per GM turn after the PC declares an action that directly repeats something they already attempted this scene, mark a Stress to give the roll disadvantage.
- **Self-Fulfilling Event — Action:** Spend a Fear and name a plausible immediate hazard already present in the scene. The PC makes an Agility or Instinct Reaction Roll (13). On a failure, take 1d8+3 damage and the hazard becomes real; on a success, take half damage or avoid the secondary complication.
- **Bad Future — Reaction:** When reduced to 3 HP or fewer, the Echo projects three possible outcomes. The GM truthfully states one danger, one opportunity, and one uncertainty in the current scene.

### Cicada Runner

**Difficulty:** 12  |  **Thresholds:** 6 / 11  |  **HP:** 3  |  **Stress:** 5
**Attack Modifier:** +0  |  **Standard Attack:** Pocket Stunner — Melee — 1d6+2 physical

**Features**
- **Always a Price — Passive:** The Runner will answer one useful question if the PC offers information, access, or a favor of comparable value.
- **Exit Clause — Reaction:** When threatened with violence, the Runner may spend a Fear to reveal a prearranged escape route or neutral-party protection. The PC can still pursue, but doing so has social consequences.

### Clause-Eater

- **Conflicting Duty — Action:** Spend a Fear. The PC makes a Presence Reaction Roll (15). On a failure, choose: mark 2 Stress, lose 1 Hope, or become Vulnerable until they publicly state which obligation they prioritize.
- **Digest the Contract — Passive:** The Clause-Eater’s Difficulty increases by +1 while within Close range of an active supernatural bargain.

### Choir Junction Intelligence
**Tier 3 Solo**

## Tier 4

### Pale Regent
**Tier 4 Solo**
A sovereign predator whose property, reputation, and bloodline function as extensions of the same body.
**Motives & Tactics:** Rule, own, preserve the Court, turn enemies into dependents
**Difficulty:** 21  |  **Thresholds:** 28 / 48  |  **HP:** 12  |  **Stress:** 9
**Attack Modifier:** +5  |  **Standard Attack:** Sovereign Strike — Melee — 4d12+12 physical

**Features**
- **The House Is Me — Passive:** While on controlled property, the Regent can perceive through mirrors, security cameras, and invited servants within Far range.
- **Command the Blood — Action:** Spend a Fear. The PC makes a Presence Reaction Roll (20). On a failure, choose: lose 2 Hope, mark 2 Stress, or obey one immediate non-suicidal command.

- **Terms of Surrender — Passive:** If the PC offers a binding concession the Regent values more than victory, it will negotiate rather than fight to death.

- **Old Bargain — Reaction:** When the PC invokes a genuine historical agreement involving the land, pause the conflict long enough for terms to be heard. If the PC has leverage, the Sovereign must negotiate in good faith.
