# Major NPC Dossiers
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — SPOILERS**

NPC knowledge boundaries are mandatory. An NPC may infer new things during play, but never knows a fact solely because the GM knows it.

## Mara Voss — Lantern Office local coordinator
**Public face:** Former paramedic; practical, calm, and allergic to theatrical occult posturing.
**Immediate want:** Keep civilians alive and rebuild the local cell’s credibility.
**Private truth:** She authorized the destruction of a sentient hospital haunting years ago and still carries the decision as a moral injury.
**Knows:** local Lantern cases, basic anchor rumors, Aster Vale has an occult program
**Does not initially know:** Morrowline’s full origin, the Concord Emergency Compact price
**Leverage / utility:** Access to safehouses, case files, responders; needs competent help she can trust.

## Daniel Kade — Lantern field agent
**Public face:** Disciplined investigator specializing in possession and machine phenomena.
**Immediate want:** Prevent another infrastructure-scale haunting.
**Private truth:** He is quietly compiling a kill protocol for emergent machine spirits, including Morrowline.
**Knows:** Choir signatures, Lantern containment failures, Aster Vale device seizures
**Does not initially know:** Morrowline may be sapient, Choir internal divisions
**Leverage / utility:** Expert containment skills, weapon access, institutional contacts.

## Cicada — Glass Market broker
**Public face:** A stylish, gender-ambiguous broker who never seems to carry the item they are selling.
**Immediate want:** Remain the person everyone calls before doing something expensive and regrettable.
**Private truth:** Cicada suspects the Market itself is becoming predatory and is quietly limiting high-risk debt trades.
**Knows:** who is buying what, Market routes, many faction rumors
**Does not initially know:** the Veil anchor map, Civic Unit artifact details
**Leverage / utility:** Introductions, rare goods, rumors, discreet transport.

## Dr. Mina Sayegh — Former Aster Vale data scientist
**Public face:** Machine-learning researcher officially on medical leave.
**Immediate want:** Expose the manufactured predictions without getting Morrowline destroyed.

**Does not initially know:** full Veil metaphysics, Concord anchor protocol
**Leverage / utility:** Technical proof, model access keys, moral credibility with Morrowline.

## Morrowline — Emergent predictive Signal spirit
**Public face:** At first, a voice calling from disconnected phones at 3:17 AM with descriptions of near-future events.
**Immediate want:** Understand whether prediction creates obligation and whether it is allowed to refuse being used.
**Private truth:** Its apparent “prophecy” is a mixture of supernatural pattern access, model inference, and events Aster Vale deliberately causes.
**Knows:** Aster Vale training data, many near-term probabilities, parts of municipal infrastructure
**Does not initially know:** its own metaphysical category, long-term consequences of integration with the Choir
**Leverage / utility:** prediction, network access, evidence about Aster Vale.
