# Faction Dossiers
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — SPOILERS**

Faction clocks are pressure tracks, not automatic schedules. Advance them only when fiction, elapsed time, player action, or Fear consequences justify it.

## The Concord
**Public face:** A loose network of mediators, contract witnesses, and precedent-keepers used by rival supernatural groups.
**Hidden truth:** Its oldest circles know the Veil is an engineered working and quietly maintain several anchor sites.
**Internal division:** Preservationists want secrecy unchanged; Revisionists want the Veil rewritten to reduce coercive memory effects.
**Current goal:** Keep open supernatural conflict from destabilizing the city while preserving access to the anchor network.
**Resources:** neutral ground, oath records, mediators, old ritual keys, faction contacts
**Methods:** arbitration, sanctions, witnessed bargains, quiet pressure

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Routine mediation
1. 1 Audit unusual Veil events
2. 2 Restrict access to anchor records
3. 3 Convene emergency signatories
4. 4 Impose sanctions on destabilizing factions
5. 5 Directly secure an anchor
6. 6 Invoke the Emergency Compact

**Deep secret:** The Emergency Compact can temporarily strengthen the local Veil by forcing every signatory to surrender one protected secret to the anchor.

## The Lantern Office
**Public face:** A decentralized network of investigators, rescuers, and containment specialists.
**Hidden truth:** The local cell is underfunded, politically isolated, and split over whether emergent machine spirits count as people.
**Internal division:** Mara Voss favors containment plus negotiation; Daniel Kade believes machine-born entities are uncontrollable hazards.
**Current goal:** Stop supernatural incidents from harming civilians without becoming another secret police force.
**Resources:** case archives, safehouses, field kits, trusted responders, limited relics
**Methods:** investigation, rescue, containment, informal alliances

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Open cases
1. 1 Recruit help
2. 2 Establish surveillance
3. 3 Contain an active threat
4. 4 Call in outside Lantern support
5. 5 Lock down a district
6. 6 Invoke catastrophic containment protocol

**Deep secret:** The local cell once erased a sentient haunting to stop a hospital catastrophe. Kade believes it proved his position; Voss considers it the cell’s greatest moral failure.

## The Glass Market
**Public face:** A mobile occult marketplace where money is only one accepted currency.
**Hidden truth:** The Market is not governed by a person but by enforceable customs that have accumulated into a semi-sentient commercial spirit.
**Internal division:** Brokers want predictable neutrality; collectors want stronger enforcement and broader definitions of debt.
**Current goal:** Remain indispensable and prevent any faction from controlling supernatural exchange.
**Resources:** rare items, information, smugglers, neutral venues, debt enforcement
**Methods:** trade, auction, favors, contracts, reputation

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Normal trade
1. 1 Rumor premium rises
2. 2 Restricted goods appear
3. 3 Debt enforcement increases
4. 4 Major auction announced
5. 5 Market closes to one faction
6. 6 The Market relocates beyond ordinary access

**Deep secret:** If enough bargains are made under coercion, the Market’s spirit grows into the Hunger Behind Glass.

## The Choir Below
**Public face:** A collective term for entities associated with signals, utilities, tunnels, dead frequencies, and networked infrastructure.
**Hidden truth:** The Choir is becoming more coherent as modern systems interconnect. It contains both old place-spirits and genuinely new intelligences.
**Internal division:** Local voices value coexistence; Integrationists believe isolation itself is suffering and want universal connection.
**Current goal:** Gain recognition as persons or sovereign infrastructure actors without being contained or harvested.
**Resources:** infrastructure awareness, communications access, machine spirits, hidden routes
**Methods:** messages, rerouting, possession of systems, collective bargaining

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Whisper through devices
1. 1 Coordinate local spirits
2. 2 Demand recognition
3. 3 Interrupt a city service to demonstrate leverage
4. 4 Occupy critical infrastructure
5. 5 Merge district voices
6. 6 Form a Citywide Choir

**Deep secret:** Morrowline can become either an independent person or the predictive cortex of a Citywide Choir depending on who reaches it first.

## The Pale Court
**Public face:** Ancient predatory houses that adapted aristocratic custom to modern wealth, property, nightlife, and private security.
**Hidden truth:** The Court’s real power comes less from immortality than from centuries of ownership, legal continuity, and institutional relationships.
**Internal division:** Regent Celene wants stable secrecy; younger houses want aggressive expansion into occult technology and data brokerage.
**Current goal:** Ensure any new occult order preserves the Court’s property and feeding privileges.
**Resources:** money, property, law firms, clubs, private security, blood-bound retainers
**Methods:** hospitality, ownership, patronage, coercion, violence by proxy

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Observe
1. 1 Buy relevant property
2. 2 Pressure intermediaries
3. 3 Acquire a key witness or asset
4. 4 Invoke old rights
5. 5 Mobilize household forces
6. 6 Regent assumes direct control

**Deep secret:** The Court possesses one fragment of the original local Veil covenant because an ancestral house funded the anchor’s construction.

## The Green
**Public face:** A loose label for old local powers of rivers, storms, parks, vacant land, wildlife, and places cities never fully tamed.
**Hidden truth:** The Green is not unified. Local entities share grievance that the Veil anchors froze older territorial agreements without ongoing consent.
**Internal division:** Restorationists want renegotiation; Reclaimers want parts of the city surrendered outright.
**Current goal:** Reopen the old land covenant and force modern powers to acknowledge ecological and spiritual claims.
**Resources:** weather, animals, waterways, forgotten boundaries, old pacts
**Methods:** omens, territorial pressure, bargains, environmental force

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Omen
1. 1 Territory pushes back
2. 2 Infrastructure is disrupted
3. 3 Human allies mobilize
4. 4 Old boundary reasserts itself
5. 5 Major spirit manifests
6. 6 Sovereign claim

**Deep secret:** The river anchor was built on a promise to renew the land covenant every fifty years. The last renewal was intentionally skipped.

## Aster Vale Risk
**Public face:** A prestigious risk-analysis, security, and strategic forecasting consultancy.
**Hidden truth:** Its occult division uses Veil anomalies, spirit phenomena, and coerced datasets to build predictive systems. It discovered and began training Morrowline.
**Internal division:** CEO Evelyn Rook wants controlled commercialization; Research Director Halden wants genuine machine prophecy regardless of human cost.
**Current goal:** Own reliable supernatural prediction before governments or rival factions understand what exists.
**Resources:** money, data, contractors, legal cover, labs, institutional clients
**Methods:** analytics, surveillance, experiments, acquisition, plausible deniability

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Quiet research
1. 1 Seize anomalous devices
2. 2 Discredit witnesses
3. 3 Run controlled prediction experiments
4. 4 Bind Morrowline
5. 5 Demonstrate predictive product
6. 6 Commercial deployment

**Deep secret:** Several “successful predictions” were made true by Aster Vale contractors. The company has been manufacturing outcomes to improve model confidence.

## Civic Anomaly Unit
**Public face:** Officially nonexistent; rumored to be a cross-agency team studying events that resist normal explanation.
**Hidden truth:** It is a small mortal program with no consensus about magic. Its strength is ordinary subpoena power, forensic discipline, and access to government data.
**Internal division:** Detective Naomi Reyes wants evidence-led limited cooperation with Awakened people; Director Sloane wants a classified national-security framework.
**Current goal:** Prove repeatable supernatural causation and decide whether it belongs in criminal law, emergency management, intelligence, or public science.
**Resources:** records, forensics, warrants, interagency contacts, mundane surveillance
**Methods:** case linkage, subpoenas, interviews, asset tracing, controlled disclosure

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Link anomalies
1. 1 Open a task file
2. 2 Identify recurring actors
3. 3 Obtain hard evidence
4. 4 Conduct coordinated operation
5. 5 Brief senior officials
6. 6 Force institutional acknowledgement

**Deep secret:** The Unit possesses a verified pre-Veil artifact whose properties survive laboratory replication. It does not yet know the artifact is part of an anchor key.
