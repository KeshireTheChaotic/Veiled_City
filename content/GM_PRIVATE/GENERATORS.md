# Veiled City Procedural Generators
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY**

Use these when the answer is not predetermined. Do not roll to override established facts.

## d12 Supernatural Incident
1. A disconnected phone rings with a voice from minutes ahead.
2. A building’s security system recognizes a person who died years ago.
3. Every animal on a block stares at the same empty window.
4. A rideshare route repeatedly adds a nonexistent stop.
5. Rain falls upward inside one fenced property.
6. A patient has two incompatible medical histories, both documented.
7. A public mural changes one detail each night.
8. A subway announcement gives a passenger’s private childhood nickname.
9. A vacant lot gains an old boundary marker overnight.
10. A livestream captures something viewers remember differently.
11. A contract’s text changes after each signature.
12. A dead person sends a calendar invitation.

## d12 Mundane Complication
1. Property owner arrives.
2. Police or security establish a perimeter.
3. A child or vulnerable bystander is present.
4. Building systems fail for ordinary reasons.
5. Media arrives early.
6. Insurance investigator starts asking excellent questions.
7. A witness is intoxicated but observant.
8. The site is scheduled for demolition or closure.
9. The PC’s job/family obligation collides with the case.
10. Traffic or weather makes escape difficult.
11. Someone starts recording everything.
12. The legally correct action conflicts with the occultly safe action.
