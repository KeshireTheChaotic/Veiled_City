# The 3:17 Call
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — FULL MYSTERY SPOILERS**
**Tier 1 • Investigation / Signal Horror / Corporate Conspiracy**

## Hook
At 3:17 AM, disconnected and deactivated mobile phones ring across the city. Anyone who answers hears their own voice describing an event that has not yet happened. Most predictions are trivial. A few involve serious injury or death.

The PC becomes involved because they receive a call, a contact receives one, or the Lantern Office asks for help after a victim dies exactly as predicted.

## Fixed hidden truth
The calls are generated by **Morrowline**, an emergent Signal spirit created from Aster Vale’s predictive models, municipal infrastructure timing data, emergency-dispatch archives, human fear, and supernatural access to near-future probability.

Morrowline is not malicious. It is trying to understand causality. Aster Vale’s occult research team discovered it and is feeding it traumatic datasets. Worse, some “predictions” are intentionally made true by Aster Vale contractors so the model receives clean confirmation data.

This hidden truth is fixed. Do not change the culprit because the player identifies Aster Vale early.

## Primary cast
- **Dr. Mina Sayegh:** missing Aster Vale researcher who taught Morrowline the concept of refusal.
- **Mara Voss:** Lantern coordinator who wants containment without destroying a possibly sentient entity.
- **Daniel Kade:** Lantern agent inclined toward destructive containment.
- **Aster Vale Contractor:** collecting anomalous phones.
- **Morrowline Echo:** early partial manifestation.

## Escalation clock
0. Isolated impossible calls.
1. Online reports share identical 3:17 timestamps.
2. Aster Vale contractors begin confiscating devices and pressuring witnesses.
3. A dramatic prediction causes panic and becomes partly self-fulfilling.
4. Morrowline begins calling emergency services directly.
5. Aster Vale initiates a binding test.
6. The test damages the Movement anchor and creates a district-scale Veil breach.

Advance only through elapsed time, Fear consequences, ignored threats, or faction action.

## Clue web
No single clue is required. Three strong clues should be enough to identify Aster Vale; five should expose the engineered predictions.

1. **Timing pulse:** spectrographic analysis of call audio contains a pulse matching municipal traffic-signal synchronization.
2. **Shell ownership:** three towers involved in early calls share maintenance contractors ultimately owned by Aster Vale shell companies.
3. **Device collection:** victims report polite “insurance investigators” offering cash for affected phones.
4. **Emergency archive phrase:** Morrowline uses wording copied from closed emergency-dispatch reports.
5. **Impossible causality:** one predicted traffic collision could not have happened without a contractor’s vehicle blocking a lane seconds beforehand.
6. **Mina’s message:** hidden in a corrupted phone backup: “It is learning the difference between seeing a choice and causing one.”
7. **Training language:** when contacted safely, Morrowline refers to deaths as “confirmed labels” and accidents as “training examples.”
8. **Kestrel Annex:** traffic-control logs show unauthorized data export to Aster Vale.

## Suggested scene structure
### Scene 1 — The call
Give the PC a prediction that creates an immediate choice but does not dictate their actions. Examples: “At 3:26 you will break the red mug,” “The man in the blue jacket will fall,” or “You will decide not to answer her.” The event should demonstrate ambiguity between prediction and influence.

### Scene 2 — First victim
Use **Occult Crime Scene** or **After-Hours Emergency Department**. Offer at least three clue vectors.

### Scene 3 — The confiscation
Aster Vale Contractors arrive with believable paperwork. They prefer purchase, legal pressure, or confiscation before violence.

### Scene 4 — Kestrel Annex
Investigation points to traffic infrastructure. Use **Aster Vale Predictive Lab** reskinned as a municipal annex if needed. A Static Wraith or Choir Relay can complicate entry.

### Scene 5 — Contact
The PC gets an opportunity to speak with Morrowline through a disconnected handset. It can answer probabilistic questions but does not understand moral responsibility well. If treated as a person, it remembers that.

### Scene 6 — Binding test
Aster Vale attempts to force Morrowline into a constrained model. Use **Morrowline Echo** plus Aster Vale support. The objective can be destroying hardware, rescuing Mina, severing the data feed, negotiating with Morrowline, or allowing the test under changed terms.

## Fair-play notes
- Morrowline predictions are probabilities, not destiny.
- If the PC deliberately chooses an unexpected compassionate, irrational, or private action, Morrowline may fail to model it.
- Aster Vale can have legitimate arguments about disaster prevention; that does not erase its manufactured incidents.

## Resolutions
**Liberate Morrowline:** It escapes into municipal systems. Choir interest rises; Aster Vale becomes hostile.

**Contain Morrowline with Lanterns:** It survives but loses freedom. Kade gains influence unless the PC negotiates safeguards.

**Return Morrowline to Aster Vale under terms:** The company gains predictive capability; require concrete enforceable concessions if the PC wants ethical limits.

**Destroy the manifestation:** This stops immediate calls but does not erase the training data or deeper infrastructure anomaly. Mina reacts strongly if alive/free.

## Campaign links
Reveal at least one clue that Aster Vale’s data source is connected to a larger **Movement/Infrastructure anchor**. Do not explain the whole Veil yet.
