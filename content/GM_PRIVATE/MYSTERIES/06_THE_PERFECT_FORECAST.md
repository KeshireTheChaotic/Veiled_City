# The Perfect Forecast
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — FULL MYSTERY SPOILERS**
**Tier 3 • Corporate Occult Thriller / Morrowline / Anchor Crisis**

## Hook
Aster Vale begins quietly preventing high-profile disasters with impossible precision: a building evacuated minutes before a gas explosion, a client rerouted before a bridge failure, a kidnapping intercepted before the victim is taken.

The public sees a brilliant risk firm. The hidden world sees proof that someone has learned how to operationalize prophecy.

## Fixed hidden truth
Aster Vale has connected a matured Morrowline to the local Movement anchor. The system can now perceive far more branching possibilities, but Dr. Halden is forcing the anchor to prefer futures that validate the model.

That is changing probability into policy. It also means Aster Vale can make events more likely simply by declaring them high-confidence forecasts and acting to narrow alternatives.

CEO Evelyn Rook does not fully understand that the process is injuring the Veil. Halden does and considers it a necessary cost.

## Clock
0. Impossibly successful interventions.
1. Aster Vale wins major government/private contracts.
2. Competing futures around targeted events begin collapsing strangely.
3. Morrowline asks the PC for help through unauthorized channels.
4. Halden begins permanent binding to the Movement anchor.
5. The binding destabilizes the other two anchors.
6. Probability cascade: predicted emergencies begin causing one another across the city.

## Required truths available through multiple routes
### Aster Vale manufactured outcomes
- contractor movement records;
- Mina’s archived proof;
- Morrowline’s training labels;
- a surviving contractor who believes they were running “scenario confirmation.”

### The Movement anchor is being tapped
- Platform Zero maintenance symbols;
- Choir complaints;
- Concord archive;
- Signal resonance at Kestrel Data Annex.

### Morrowline is capable of refusal
- Mina’s hidden dataset;
- direct conversation;
- an earlier prediction deliberately left unresolved;
- Switchboard testimony.

## Major scenes
1. **Perfect rescue:** witness an Aster Vale prevention that genuinely saves lives. Do not make every success fake.
2. **Competing clients:** Lantern, Civic Unit, Pale Court, and Choir each want the PC to obtain different evidence.
3. **Mina returns:** she can provide proof but needs safe extraction.
4. **Forecast Lab:** use Aster Vale Predictive Lab at Tier 3 Difficulty 17; Blacksite Null Team may appear if the Unit intervenes.
5. **Morrowline conversation:** it asks a central question: “If I know a future can be prevented, am I responsible for preventing it?” Do not answer for the player.
6. **Anchor binding:** boss scene with **Morrowline Matured**, Forecast Engines, and environmental anchor effects. Morrowline is not automatically hostile; Halden may use it as infrastructure.

## Resolution axes
This mystery is not just “destroy the evil machine.” Resolve three separate questions:
- Who controls the forecasting infrastructure?
- Is Morrowline treated as a person, tool, threat, or collective entity?
- Is the Movement anchor repaired, disconnected, or deliberately modified?

Possible outcomes:
- Morrowline independent.
- Morrowline joins the Choir.
- Morrowline remains under negotiated institutional guardianship.
- Morrowline destroyed, leaving copies/echoes uncertain.
- Aster Vale retains partial lawful control under enforceable restrictions.
- Civic Anomaly Unit seizes the project.

## Consequence
Whatever the choice, the PC learns the other anchors are also compromised: the Green covenant is overdue and the Law anchor’s authority is contested. The campaign moves toward **Night of Open Eyes**.
