# Saint of the Red Line
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — FULL MYSTERY SPOILERS**
**Tier 2 • Transit Spirit / Public Safety / Personhood**

## Hook
A pale figure appears on subway platforms seconds before major accidents. Witnesses describe different faces but the same red transit jacket. The figure has prevented several deaths. A transit contractor quietly hires occult specialists to capture it after one intervention shuts down service for hours.

## Fixed hidden truth
The “Saint” is a composite spirit formed from decades of small acts of commuter care: strangers pulling one another away from tracks, operators stopping trains, first responders, memorial offerings, and public-safety rituals. It is linked to the Movement anchor but is not part of the original anchor system.

Aster Vale wants to capture the Saint because its anticipatory behavior could improve Morrowline. The transit authority’s mundane leadership does not know this; a contractor embedded by Aster Vale framed the spirit as the source of the accidents.

## Clock
0. Protective apparitions.
1. Service disruptions draw media attention.
2. Contractor deploys traps and sensors.
3. Saint is partially contained; accidents increase because its interventions stop.
4. Aster Vale moves the captured fragment.
5. Transit spirits and Choir entities retaliate across the line.
6. Movement anchor destabilizes and Platform Zero becomes publicly accessible.

## Clues
1. Accident statistics show the Saint appears before danger rather than causing it.
2. The same contractor services both new anomaly sensors and Aster Vale facilities.
3. Memorial offerings at Red Line stations resonate with the Saint.
4. Switchboard knows the Saint refuses to join the Choir because it identifies with commuters, not infrastructure.
5. A trap design uses the same predictive-binding geometry seen in Aster Vale work.
6. Transit workers have stories of “someone in red” predating Aster Vale by decades.
7. Captured Saint fragments repeatedly attempt to trigger emergency-stop protocols.
8. Platform Zero contains an old anchor-maintenance symbol matching the Saint’s aura.

## Scenes
- Near accident and manifestation.
- Interviews with operator, commuter, and maintenance worker.
- Investigation of sensor contractor.
- Platform Zero traversal.
- Capture operation: Aster Vale Forecast Engine + Ritual Enforcement Team.
- Choice whether to free, negotiate with, or formally recognize the Saint.

## Resolutions
**Recognition compact:** the Saint becomes an acknowledged transit guardian under negotiated rules; Choir approval rises.

**Lantern containment:** stable but ethically ambiguous; Kade supports it.

**Aster Vale capture:** prediction project advances significantly.

**Disperse the Saint:** accidents stop being supernaturally influenced, but the Movement anchor loses a stabilizing emergent intelligence.

## Campaign link
The PC can learn that the Movement anchor is not just a machine—it has accumulated new spirits around it, raising the question of whether repairing the Veil means preserving or suppressing those new beings.
