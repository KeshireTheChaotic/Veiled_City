# Dead Letter Office
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — FULL MYSTERY SPOILERS**
**Tier 1 • Ghost Story / Communication / Regret**

## Hook
Undeliverable letters appear in occupied homes without postage or entry. Some are from dead relatives. Some are written by missing people. One arrives for the PC or a contact and contains a private fact no stranger should know.

## Fixed hidden truth
A minor spirit called **Return to Sender** formed around a decommissioned postal sorting facility where decades of undelivered mail were stored and eventually destroyed. It believes every message must reach “the person it belongs to,” but it understands emotional ownership better than legal addresses.

A Glass Market broker has begun feeding it stolen confessions and dead letters, using the spirit to locate people and extract secrets. The spirit is not intentionally harmful; the broker’s use is teaching it that secrecy is a delivery error.

## Clock
0. Curious letters.
1. Letters reveal embarrassing secrets.
2. Missing-person messages appear.
3. The spirit begins delivering unsent drafts and deleted messages.
4. People act violently or desperately on private revelations.
5. The broker attempts to bind Return to Sender as an information service.
6. Every “undelivered” secret in a district begins seeking a recipient.

## Clues
1. Paper fibers come from multiple decades but carry the same dust/pollen signature.
2. A stamp cancellation references a postal facility closed twenty years ago.
3. One letter contains a phrase copied from a deleted digital draft, proving the spirit now reaches beyond paper.
4. Glass Market Scrip residue appears on a bundle.
5. Cicada can identify a broker called **Moth Receipt** who recently bought “undelivered intention.”
6. Old postal records show a warehouse fire destroyed millions of dead letters at once.
7. The spirit reacts positively to proper addresses, apologies, and completed deliveries.
8. The broker’s target list includes people tied to Aster Vale and the Civic Anomaly Unit.

## Scenes
- **Impossible delivery:** establish the unsettling but potentially compassionate side of the spirit.
- **Trace the paper:** archive, postal worker, forensic lab, or occult resonance.
- **Glass Market inquiry:** information can be bought without violence.
- **Old sorting facility:** use an Exploration environment at Difficulty 11; Static Wraiths can be re-skinned as Letter Echoes.
- **Binding attempt:** the broker tries to turn the spirit into a permanent intelligence asset.

## Moral question
Does a message “belong” to its intended recipient if the sender chose not to send it? The spirit genuinely needs an answer. The PC can influence its future behavior by establishing a rule it understands.

## Resolutions
- Establish a consent rule for delivery and let the spirit continue existing.
- Bind it to a sanctuary/archive with ethical constraints.
- Drive it away from human communications.
- Sell or surrender it to a faction, with obvious future consequences.

## Campaign link
One stolen letter references a Civic Anomaly Unit artifact transfer or an Aster Vale shell company, whichever best serves current pacing.
