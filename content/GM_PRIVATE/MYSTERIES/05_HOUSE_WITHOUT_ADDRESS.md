# House Without Address
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — FULL MYSTERY SPOILERS**
**Tier 2 • Property Horror / Identity / Hidden Refuge**

## Hook
Navigation apps periodically route drivers to an address that does not exist. Packages are delivered and signed for. Utility payments clear. Property taxes are current. People who try to visit report circling the block and forgetting why they came.

Then a missing person sends the PC a photo from inside the house.

## Fixed hidden truth
**House 404** is a real property whose civic identity was deliberately severed from its physical location decades ago using Law-anchor magic. It was created as a refuge for supernatural witnesses during an earlier political purge.

The refuge’s caretaker recently died. Without a recognized caretaker, the protective magic has begun “collecting” people who are trying to disappear, including some who never consented.

A **Name-Eater** has nested in the gap between the property and its records. It is worsening the effect and feeding on the social connections of residents.

## Clock
0. Navigation anomalies.
1. Packages and records point to contradictory locations.
2. A missing person contacts the PC.
3. Residents inside begin forgetting outside relationships.
4. Name-Eater consumes the caretaker’s identity entirely.
5. House starts drawing anyone nearby who desperately wishes to disappear.
6. House severs from the city and takes all current residents with it.

## Clues
1. Property taxes are paid from an old trust with no current trustee.
2. Utility usage maps to infrastructure but not cadastral property maps.
3. Delivery photos show different exteriors with identical porch details.
4. Samir Patel finds a legal description that references “the parcel held outside ordinary notice.”
5. Owen Pike identifies the former caretaker among recent unclaimed dead.
6. The missing person remembers relationships less clearly each day.
7. Threshold Sense reveals a doorway that exists only when approached while sincerely seeking refuge.
8. Name-Eater activity leaves blank spaces in photographs, contact lists, and witness sentences.

## Scenes
- Trace impossible deliveries.
- Research title/trust history in Municipal Archive.
- Find deceased caretaker’s body/records.
- Enter House 404 by meeting its emotional threshold condition.
- Social scene with residents: some want to leave, some desperately do not.
- Confront Name-Eater while preserving the house’s original protective purpose.

## Resolutions
**Appoint a caretaker:** restore the refuge with consent rules and a living/recognized steward.

**Reattach the address:** make the house an ordinary property again, sacrificing supernatural concealment.

**Transfer to Lantern/Sanctuary:** creates a valuable safehouse but gives an institution power over refuge access.

**Destroy the severance:** drives out the Name-Eater but may expose residents to enemies they were hiding from.

## Campaign link
The house’s creation documents prove the Law/Ownership anchor can alter recognition without erasing material reality. This is an important precedent for later arguments about rewriting the Veil.
