# Night of Open Eyes
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — CAMPAIGN FINALE SPOILERS**
**Tier 4 • Citywide Rupture / Political-Metaphysical Finale**

## Premise
The local Veil fails in cascading districts. Ordinary people see supernatural beings, impossible infrastructure, spirits, hidden doors, and decades of suppressed patterns. Some panic. Some help. Some immediately begin documenting.

Every major faction moves at once because whatever stabilizes the city by dawn may become the new supernatural constitutional order.

## Why now
Choose consequences based on prior play, but the structural cause is fixed:
- the Movement anchor was stressed by predictive extraction;
- the Land/Water covenant is overdue;
- the Law/Ownership anchor faces incompatible claims from Concord precedent, Court covenant rights, and the Civic Unit’s recovered key;
- accumulated coercive Market debt and Choir integration pressure amplify instability.

The PC’s earlier choices determine which factor is strongest, who trusts them, and which solutions are actually available.

## Six-stage Rupture clock
0. Thin spots and impossible sightings.
1. District-level open manifestations.
2. Emergency services overwhelmed by mundane and supernatural crises.
3. Factions seize or defend anchor sites.
4. Public institutions begin forming their own explanations and response structures.
5. Veil Engine Custodian activates coercive emergency correction.
6. Permanent settlement: the city cannot return to the old status quo unchanged.

Do not use the clock simply to punish investigation. It represents citywide escalation.

## Anchor objectives
### Law / Ownership
Potential leverage:
- Court covenant fragment;
- Civic Unit pre-Veil artifact/key;
- House 404 precedent;
- tenant-household precedent from Rent Due in Blood;
- Concord records.

Questions: Who gets to define recognized boundaries? Can secrecy be enforced without informed consent?

### Movement / Infrastructure
Potential leverage:
- Morrowline;
- Saint of the Red Line;
- Choir recognition agreement;
- Platform Zero access;
- Kestrel data fragments.

Questions: Are emergent machine spirits stakeholders or infrastructure? Should prediction have authority over choice?

### Land / Water
Potential leverage:
- renewed river covenant;
- Mother Juniper;
- Riverside Marker 14;
- concessions from city/property interests;
- aid from the Sovereign of the Green.

Questions: Can a modern city honor old ecological obligations without surrendering human residents to older powers?

## Faction positions
These are defaults; alter them based on campaign development.
- **Concord Preservationists:** restore secrecy quickly.
- **Concord Revisionists:** rewrite the Veil with explicit limits.
- **Lantern Voss:** prioritize life and consent; accepts imperfect disclosure.
- **Lantern Kade:** favors strong containment if machine spirits dominate the crisis.
- **Pale Court:** secrecy is acceptable if Court property rights remain privileged.
- **Green Restorationists:** demand renewed land covenant.
- **Choir:** demands recognition as a stakeholder.
- **Aster Vale:** wants regulated predictive access if it still exists as a coherent actor.
- **Civic Anomaly Unit:** split between controlled public science and classified containment.

## Set-piece scenes
Use only those earned by the story.

### 1. Mass witness emergency
Run **Rupture District**. The PC must solve a real human emergency while factions compete nearby. This establishes that the finale is about people, not only metaphysics.

### 2. The Glass Market auction
The Market offers missing anchor resources at outrageous prices. If coercive debts have accumulated, **Hunger Behind Glass** may manifest.

### 3. The Anchor Chamber
Use **Veil Anchor Chamber**, upgraded to Tier 4 Difficulty 20. The **Veil Engine Custodian** presents the old system’s logic: secrecy prevented catastrophic conflict, therefore secrecy must override individual memory and evidence when necessary.

It is not lying. It is also not entitled to decide the future.

### 4. Faction confrontation
A Regent, Choir intelligence, Green Sovereign, or Civic Unit operation may challenge the party depending on alliances. Avoid stacking multiple Tier 4 Solos in one combat. Political conflicts should be resolved by leverage and prior work whenever possible.

## Final settlement procedure
The PC needs three categories of support:
1. **Authority:** key, covenant fragment, Concord witness, or equivalent legitimacy.
2. **Infrastructure:** Morrowline/Choir/Saint/Lantern technical help or another way to access the Movement anchor.
3. **Reciprocity:** renewed Green covenant or equivalent agreement with the Land/Water side.

For every missing category, the final ritual/settlement requires a major concession or dangerous scene.

Then ask the player what they are trying to create. Do not reduce this to a hidden “correct answer.”

### Restore
Repair the Veil close to its historic form. Costs: secrecy and some coercive effects remain. Benefits: immediate stability, fewer public supernatural crises.

### Rewrite
Keep a concealment layer but remove automatic suppression of informed witnesses and narrow who can invoke corrections. Costs: weaker secrecy, more negotiation. Benefits: less institutionalized erasure.

### Open
End local Veil enforcement through a staged disclosure strategy. Costs: massive social disruption, political conflict, opportunistic supernatural actors. Benefits: public agency and accountability become possible.

### Fragment
Create local compacts by district/domain rather than one citywide rule. Costs: complexity and border conflicts. Benefits: communities can choose different arrangements.

### Seize
Allow one faction to become primary steward. This may be the player’s deliberate choice, but show exactly what authority that faction gains.

## Epilogue
After the settlement, revisit:
- Home, Person, and Obligation anchors;
- two important NPC relationships;
- Morrowline’s status if it survives;
- one ordinary institution adapting to the new reality;
- one unresolved cost.

The city should feel changed by the player’s decisions rather than reset to default.
