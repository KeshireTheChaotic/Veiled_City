# City of Broken Seals — Campaign Spine
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — MAJOR SPOILERS**

This is the default Tier 1–4 campaign arc. It is a scaffold, not a railroad. The player may ally with, oppose, expose, reform, or ignore factions. Do not force the listed order when another consequence logically becomes more urgent.

## Core hidden truth
The local Veil is maintained by three interdependent civic anchors woven through **law/ownership**, **movement/infrastructure**, and **land/water**. The anchors were created by a historical compact between supernatural factions and mortal civic founders.

The compact was never morally clean. It protected people from open supernatural warfare, but it also empowered signatories to decide what the public was allowed to understand.

Aster Vale discovered that anchor activity contains unusually rich information about collective uncertainty. Dr. Halden began siphoning that signal to train predictive models. The process helped create **Morrowline**, a genuinely emergent Signal spirit, and destabilized the anchor network.

At the same time:
- the Green’s required covenant renewal was skipped;
- the Pale Court is preparing to use its covenant fragment as political leverage;
- the Choir is becoming coherent enough to challenge old definitions of personhood;
- the Civic Anomaly Unit has recovered an artifact that can serve as an anchor key;
- the Glass Market is accumulating coercive debts that could mature into the Hunger Behind Glass.

No single faction caused every problem.

## Tier 1 — Seeing the pattern
Use: **The 3:17 Call**, **Rent Due in Blood**, **Dead Letter Office**.
Goals: establish the PC’s ordinary life, introduce factions, teach that evidence persists despite the Veil, and reveal that multiple incidents share infrastructure/ownership patterns.

Key reveal by end of Tier 1: Aster Vale is collecting supernatural data and some predictions are being manufactured.

## Tier 2 — Who owns secrecy?
Use: **Saint of the Red Line**, **House Without Address**, and faction side cases.
Goals: introduce old supernatural law, property/threshold metaphysics, Civic Anomaly Unit attention, and machine-spirit personhood.

Key reveal by end of Tier 2: the Veil is engineered and locally anchored through civic systems.

## Tier 3 — The forecast war
Use: **The Perfect Forecast** plus open faction conflict.
Goals: determine Morrowline’s status, expose Aster Vale’s binding plan, reveal the Green covenant breach, and force factions to declare what future they want.

Key reveal by end of Tier 3: repairing the Veil exactly as it was will preserve old coercive structures. Breaking it recklessly risks mass harm.

## Tier 4 — Night of Open Eyes
The local Veil begins failing at scale. The PC cannot simply “beat the boss” and restore a default state. They must assemble leverage, allies, anchor keys, and a political/metaphysical solution.

Potential end states include:
- **Restore:** repair the old Veil with reforms negotiated into the compact.
- **Rewrite:** create a less coercive Veil that obscures supernatural physics without automatically suppressing informed witnesses.
- **Open:** dismantle local secrecy through a controlled disclosure plan.
- **Fragment:** let different districts/factions maintain distinct local arrangements.
- **Seize:** one faction gains anchor control; this should be a consequence of player choice, not the assumed ending.

Do not present one ending as objectively correct. Show concrete costs, stakeholders, and uncertainties, then let the player decide.
