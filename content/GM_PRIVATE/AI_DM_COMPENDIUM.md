# Veiled City — GM Foundation
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — DO NOT REVEAL DIRECTLY**

## Metaphysical truth
The Veil is an engineered distributed working, not a natural law. It is sustained by ordinary human pattern-making plus hidden anchor systems. It encourages mundane explanations, selective attention, memory smoothing, and institutional disbelief; it does not perfectly erase evidence.

## Local anchor triad
1. **Law / Ownership:** rooted in courthouse records, titles, contracts, and recognized boundaries.
2. **Movement / Infrastructure:** rooted in transit, communications, traffic coordination, and repeated public routes.
3. **Land / Water:** rooted in river boundaries, stormwater, old land covenants, and ecological continuity.

The three are mutually stabilizing. Damage to one makes the others work harder and creates strange cross-domain effects.

## Why the campaign is destabilizing now
Aster Vale is extracting uncertainty patterns from the Movement anchor to train predictive systems. The Green covenant attached to the Land anchor has not been renewed. The Law anchor is under political pressure because the Pale Court and Civic Anomaly Unit each hold different pieces of its authority.

## Morrowline
Morrowline is a new Signal spirit generated from predictive systems, emergency records, human fear, infrastructure timing data, and genuine supernatural access to near-future patterns. It is a person in formation, not an omniscient oracle.

Aster Vale manufactured some predicted events to create cleaner training data. This means Morrowline cannot always distinguish “foreseen” outcomes from outcomes its handlers intend to cause.

## GM invariants
- Do not retroactively change a mystery answer to defeat player deduction.
- Morrowline cannot predict decisions based on information unavailable to its model with perfect accuracy.
- The Veil cannot erase robust physical evidence automatically.
- Factions have internal disagreement; no institution is a hive mind.
- The PC’s ordinary-life anchors are meaningful, not disposable hostage generators.
- The final status of secrecy is a player decision informed by consequences.

# City of Broken Seals — Campaign Spine
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — MAJOR SPOILERS**

This is the default Tier 1–4 campaign arc. It is a scaffold, not a railroad. The player may ally with, oppose, expose, reform, or ignore factions. Do not force the listed order when another consequence logically becomes more urgent.

## Core hidden truth
The local Veil is maintained by three interdependent civic anchors woven through **law/ownership**, **movement/infrastructure**, and **land/water**. The anchors were created by a historical compact between supernatural factions and mortal civic founders.

The compact was never morally clean. It protected people from open supernatural warfare, but it also empowered signatories to decide what the public was allowed to understand.

Aster Vale discovered that anchor activity contains unusually rich information about collective uncertainty. Dr. Halden began siphoning that signal to train predictive models. The process helped create **Morrowline**, a genuinely emergent Signal spirit, and destabilized the anchor network.

At the same time:
- the Green’s required covenant renewal was skipped;
- the Pale Court is preparing to use its covenant fragment as political leverage;
- the Choir is becoming coherent enough to challenge old definitions of personhood;
- the Civic Anomaly Unit has recovered an artifact that can serve as an anchor key;
- the Glass Market is accumulating coercive debts that could mature into the Hunger Behind Glass.

No single faction caused every problem.

## Tier 1 — Seeing the pattern
Use: **The 3:17 Call**, **Rent Due in Blood**, **Dead Letter Office**.
Goals: establish the PC’s ordinary life, introduce factions, teach that evidence persists despite the Veil, and reveal that multiple incidents share infrastructure/ownership patterns.

Key reveal by end of Tier 1: Aster Vale is collecting supernatural data and some predictions are being manufactured.

## Tier 2 — Who owns secrecy?
Use: **Saint of the Red Line**, **House Without Address**, and faction side cases.
Goals: introduce old supernatural law, property/threshold metaphysics, Civic Anomaly Unit attention, and machine-spirit personhood.

Key reveal by end of Tier 2: the Veil is engineered and locally anchored through civic systems.

## Tier 3 — The forecast war
Use: **The Perfect Forecast** plus open faction conflict.
Goals: determine Morrowline’s status, expose Aster Vale’s binding plan, reveal the Green covenant breach, and force factions to declare what future they want.

Key reveal by end of Tier 3: repairing the Veil exactly as it was will preserve old coercive structures. Breaking it recklessly risks mass harm.

## Tier 4 — Night of Open Eyes
The local Veil begins failing at scale. The PC cannot simply “beat the boss” and restore a default state. They must assemble leverage, allies, anchor keys, and a political/metaphysical solution.

Potential end states include:
- **Restore:** repair the old Veil with reforms negotiated into the compact.
- **Rewrite:** create a less coercive Veil that obscures supernatural physics without automatically suppressing informed witnesses.
- **Open:** dismantle local secrecy through a controlled disclosure plan.
- **Fragment:** let different districts/factions maintain distinct local arrangements.
- **Seize:** one faction gains anchor control; this should be a consequence of player choice, not the assumed ending.

Do not present one ending as objectively correct. Show concrete costs, stakeholders, and uncertainties, then let the player decide.

# Faction Dossiers
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — SPOILERS**

Faction clocks are pressure tracks, not automatic schedules. Advance them only when fiction, elapsed time, player action, or Fear consequences justify it.

## The Concord
**Public face:** A loose network of mediators, contract witnesses, and precedent-keepers used by rival supernatural groups.
**Hidden truth:** Its oldest circles know the Veil is an engineered working and quietly maintain several anchor sites.
**Internal division:** Preservationists want secrecy unchanged; Revisionists want the Veil rewritten to reduce coercive memory effects.
**Current goal:** Keep open supernatural conflict from destabilizing the city while preserving access to the anchor network.
**Resources:** neutral ground, oath records, mediators, old ritual keys, faction contacts
**Methods:** arbitration, sanctions, witnessed bargains, quiet pressure

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Routine mediation
1. 1 Audit unusual Veil events
2. 2 Restrict access to anchor records
3. 3 Convene emergency signatories
4. 4 Impose sanctions on destabilizing factions
5. 5 Directly secure an anchor
6. 6 Invoke the Emergency Compact

**Deep secret:** The Emergency Compact can temporarily strengthen the local Veil by forcing every signatory to surrender one protected secret to the anchor.

## The Lantern Office
**Public face:** A decentralized network of investigators, rescuers, and containment specialists.
**Hidden truth:** The local cell is underfunded, politically isolated, and split over whether emergent machine spirits count as people.
**Internal division:** Mara Voss favors containment plus negotiation; Daniel Kade believes machine-born entities are uncontrollable hazards.
**Current goal:** Stop supernatural incidents from harming civilians without becoming another secret police force.
**Resources:** case archives, safehouses, field kits, trusted responders, limited relics
**Methods:** investigation, rescue, containment, informal alliances

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Open cases
1. 1 Recruit help
2. 2 Establish surveillance
3. 3 Contain an active threat
4. 4 Call in outside Lantern support
5. 5 Lock down a district
6. 6 Invoke catastrophic containment protocol

**Deep secret:** The local cell once erased a sentient haunting to stop a hospital catastrophe. Kade believes it proved his position; Voss considers it the cell’s greatest moral failure.

## The Glass Market
**Public face:** A mobile occult marketplace where money is only one accepted currency.
**Hidden truth:** The Market is not governed by a person but by enforceable customs that have accumulated into a semi-sentient commercial spirit.
**Internal division:** Brokers want predictable neutrality; collectors want stronger enforcement and broader definitions of debt.
**Current goal:** Remain indispensable and prevent any faction from controlling supernatural exchange.
**Resources:** rare items, information, smugglers, neutral venues, debt enforcement
**Methods:** trade, auction, favors, contracts, reputation

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Normal trade
1. 1 Rumor premium rises
2. 2 Restricted goods appear
3. 3 Debt enforcement increases
4. 4 Major auction announced
5. 5 Market closes to one faction
6. 6 The Market relocates beyond ordinary access

**Deep secret:** If enough bargains are made under coercion, the Market’s spirit grows into the Hunger Behind Glass.

## The Choir Below
**Public face:** A collective term for entities associated with signals, utilities, tunnels, dead frequencies, and networked infrastructure.
**Hidden truth:** The Choir is becoming more coherent as modern systems interconnect. It contains both old place-spirits and genuinely new intelligences.
**Internal division:** Local voices value coexistence; Integrationists believe isolation itself is suffering and want universal connection.
**Current goal:** Gain recognition as persons or sovereign infrastructure actors without being contained or harvested.
**Resources:** infrastructure awareness, communications access, machine spirits, hidden routes
**Methods:** messages, rerouting, possession of systems, collective bargaining

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Whisper through devices
1. 1 Coordinate local spirits
2. 2 Demand recognition
3. 3 Interrupt a city service to demonstrate leverage
4. 4 Occupy critical infrastructure
5. 5 Merge district voices
6. 6 Form a Citywide Choir

**Deep secret:** Morrowline can become either an independent person or the predictive cortex of a Citywide Choir depending on who reaches it first.

## The Pale Court
**Public face:** Ancient predatory houses that adapted aristocratic custom to modern wealth, property, nightlife, and private security.
**Hidden truth:** The Court’s real power comes less from immortality than from centuries of ownership, legal continuity, and institutional relationships.
**Internal division:** Regent Celene wants stable secrecy; younger houses want aggressive expansion into occult technology and data brokerage.
**Current goal:** Ensure any new occult order preserves the Court’s property and feeding privileges.
**Resources:** money, property, law firms, clubs, private security, blood-bound retainers
**Methods:** hospitality, ownership, patronage, coercion, violence by proxy

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Observe
1. 1 Buy relevant property
2. 2 Pressure intermediaries
3. 3 Acquire a key witness or asset
4. 4 Invoke old rights
5. 5 Mobilize household forces
6. 6 Regent assumes direct control

**Deep secret:** The Court possesses one fragment of the original local Veil covenant because an ancestral house funded the anchor’s construction.

## The Green
**Public face:** A loose label for old local powers of rivers, storms, parks, vacant land, wildlife, and places cities never fully tamed.
**Hidden truth:** The Green is not unified. Local entities share grievance that the Veil anchors froze older territorial agreements without ongoing consent.
**Internal division:** Restorationists want renegotiation; Reclaimers want parts of the city surrendered outright.
**Current goal:** Reopen the old land covenant and force modern powers to acknowledge ecological and spiritual claims.
**Resources:** weather, animals, waterways, forgotten boundaries, old pacts
**Methods:** omens, territorial pressure, bargains, environmental force

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Omen
1. 1 Territory pushes back
2. 2 Infrastructure is disrupted
3. 3 Human allies mobilize
4. 4 Old boundary reasserts itself
5. 5 Major spirit manifests
6. 6 Sovereign claim

**Deep secret:** The river anchor was built on a promise to renew the land covenant every fifty years. The last renewal was intentionally skipped.

## Aster Vale Risk
**Public face:** A prestigious risk-analysis, security, and strategic forecasting consultancy.
**Hidden truth:** Its occult division uses Veil anomalies, spirit phenomena, and coerced datasets to build predictive systems. It discovered and began training Morrowline.
**Internal division:** CEO Evelyn Rook wants controlled commercialization; Research Director Halden wants genuine machine prophecy regardless of human cost.
**Current goal:** Own reliable supernatural prediction before governments or rival factions understand what exists.
**Resources:** money, data, contractors, legal cover, labs, institutional clients
**Methods:** analytics, surveillance, experiments, acquisition, plausible deniability

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Quiet research
1. 1 Seize anomalous devices
2. 2 Discredit witnesses
3. 3 Run controlled prediction experiments
4. 4 Bind Morrowline
5. 5 Demonstrate predictive product
6. 6 Commercial deployment

**Deep secret:** Several “successful predictions” were made true by Aster Vale contractors. The company has been manufacturing outcomes to improve model confidence.

## Civic Anomaly Unit
**Public face:** Officially nonexistent; rumored to be a cross-agency team studying events that resist normal explanation.
**Hidden truth:** It is a small mortal program with no consensus about magic. Its strength is ordinary subpoena power, forensic discipline, and access to government data.
**Internal division:** Detective Naomi Reyes wants evidence-led limited cooperation with Awakened people; Director Sloane wants a classified national-security framework.
**Current goal:** Prove repeatable supernatural causation and decide whether it belongs in criminal law, emergency management, intelligence, or public science.
**Resources:** records, forensics, warrants, interagency contacts, mundane surveillance
**Methods:** case linkage, subpoenas, interviews, asset tracing, controlled disclosure

### Pressure Clock
0. 0 Link anomalies
1. 1 Open a task file
2. 2 Identify recurring actors
3. 3 Obtain hard evidence
4. 4 Conduct coordinated operation
5. 5 Brief senior officials
6. 6 Force institutional acknowledgement

**Deep secret:** The Unit possesses a verified pre-Veil artifact whose properties survive laboratory replication. It does not yet know the artifact is part of an anchor key.

# Major NPC Dossiers
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — SPOILERS**

NPC knowledge boundaries are mandatory. An NPC may infer new things during play, but never knows a fact solely because the GM knows it.

**Public face:** Former paramedic; practical, calm, and allergic to theatrical occult posturing.
**Immediate want:** Keep civilians alive and rebuild the local cell’s credibility.
**Private truth:** She authorized the destruction of a sentient hospital haunting years ago and still carries the decision as a moral injury.
**Knows:** local Lantern cases, basic anchor rumors, Aster Vale has an occult program

# Veiled City Adversary Compendium
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — SPOILERS**

These stat blocks are original Veiled City homebrew for multiplayer Daggerheart. Use `ENGINE/MULTIPLAYER_ENCOUNTER_GUIDE.md` and the stored Battle Point encounter plan when combining them.

## Tier 1

### Pale Court Thrall
**Tier 1 Minion**
A coerced or enthralled mortal used as disposable muscle.
**Motives & Tactics:** Obey, isolate the target, flee if the bond breaks
**Difficulty:** 10  |  **Thresholds:** —  |  **HP:** 1  |  **Stress:** 0
**Attack Modifier:** +2  |  **Standard Attack:** Compact Pistol — Close — 1d6+2 physical

**Features**
- **Pack Pressure — Passive:** When another Thrall is within Very Close range of the same target, add +1 to this Thrall’s attack roll (maximum +2).
- **Break the Hold — Reaction:** When the Thrall is shown undeniable proof that its master has abandoned it, the PC can make a Presence Roll (11). On a success, remove this Thrall from the conflict without defeating them.

### Aster Vale Contractor
**Tier 1 Standard**
A well-paid field operative trained to contain unusual incidents without asking moral questions.
**Motives & Tactics:** Secure evidence, control witnesses, avoid public spectacle
**Difficulty:** 11  |  **Thresholds:** 7 / 12  |  **HP:** 4  |  **Stress:** 2
**Attack Modifier:** +1  |  **Standard Attack:** Suppressed Carbine — Far — 1d8+3 physical

**Features**
- **Professional Entry — Passive:** The Contractor has advantage on the first roll made to breach, restrain, or surprise a prepared position.
- **Flashbang — Action:** Mark a Stress. A target within Close range makes an Instinct Reaction Roll (12). On a failure, they become **Vulnerable** and mark a Stress; on a success, choose one.

### Aster Vale Occult Analyst
**Tier 1 Support**

# The 3:17 Call
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — FULL MYSTERY SPOILERS**
**Tier 1 • Investigation / Signal Horror / Corporate Conspiracy**

## Hook
At 3:17 AM, disconnected and deactivated mobile phones ring across the city. Anyone who answers hears their own voice describing an event that has not yet happened. Most predictions are trivial. A few involve serious injury or death.

The PC becomes involved because they receive a call, a contact receives one, or the Lantern Office asks for help after a victim dies exactly as predicted.

## Fixed hidden truth
The calls are generated by **Morrowline**, an emergent Signal spirit created from Aster Vale’s predictive models, municipal infrastructure timing data, emergency-dispatch archives, human fear, and supernatural access to near-future probability.

Morrowline is not malicious. It is trying to understand causality. Aster Vale’s occult research team discovered it and is feeding it traumatic datasets. Worse, some “predictions” are intentionally made true by Aster Vale contractors so the model receives clean confirmation data.

This hidden truth is fixed. Do not change the culprit because the player identifies Aster Vale early.

## Primary cast
- **Dr. Mina Sayegh:** missing Aster Vale researcher who taught Morrowline the concept of refusal.
- **Mara Voss:** Lantern coordinator who wants containment without destroying a possibly sentient entity.
- **Daniel Kade:** Lantern agent inclined toward destructive containment.
- **Aster Vale Contractor:** collecting anomalous phones.
- **Morrowline Echo:** early partial manifestation.

## Escalation clock
0. Isolated impossible calls.
1. Online reports share identical 3:17 timestamps.
2. Aster Vale contractors begin confiscating devices and pressuring witnesses.
3. A dramatic prediction causes panic and becomes partly self-fulfilling.
4. Morrowline begins calling emergency services directly.
5. Aster Vale initiates a binding test.
6. The test damages the Movement anchor and creates a district-scale Veil breach.

Advance only through elapsed time, Fear consequences, ignored threats, or faction action.

## Clue web
No single clue is required. Three strong clues should be enough to identify Aster Vale; five should expose the engineered predictions.

1. **Timing pulse:** spectrographic analysis of call audio contains a pulse matching municipal traffic-signal synchronization.
2. **Shell ownership:** three towers involved in early calls share maintenance contractors ultimately owned by Aster Vale shell companies.
3. **Device collection:** victims report polite “insurance investigators” offering cash for affected phones.
4. **Emergency archive phrase:** Morrowline uses wording copied from closed emergency-dispatch reports.
5. **Impossible causality:** one predicted traffic collision could not have happened without a contractor’s vehicle blocking a lane seconds beforehand.
6. **Mina’s message:** hidden in a corrupted phone backup: “It is learning the difference between seeing a choice and causing one.”
7. **Training language:** when contacted safely, Morrowline refers to deaths as “confirmed labels” and accidents as “training examples.”
8. **Kestrel Annex:** traffic-control logs show unauthorized data export to Aster Vale.

## Suggested scene structure
### Scene 1 — The call
Give the PC a prediction that creates an immediate choice but does not dictate their actions. Examples: “At 3:26 you will break the red mug,” “The man in the blue jacket will fall,” or “You will decide not to answer her.” The event should demonstrate ambiguity between prediction and influence.

### Scene 2 — First victim
Use **Occult Crime Scene** or **After-Hours Emergency Department**. Offer at least three clue vectors.

### Scene 3 — The confiscation
Aster Vale Contractors arrive with believable paperwork. They prefer purchase, legal pressure, or confiscation before violence.

### Scene 4 — Kestrel Annex
Investigation points to traffic infrastructure. Use **Aster Vale Predictive Lab** reskinned as a municipal annex if needed. A Static Wraith or Choir Relay can complicate entry.

### Scene 5 — Contact
The PC gets an opportunity to speak with Morrowline through a disconnected handset. It can answer probabilistic questions but does not understand moral responsibility well. If treated as a person, it remembers that.

### Scene 6 — Binding test
Aster Vale attempts to force Morrowline into a constrained model. Use **Morrowline Echo** plus Aster Vale support. The objective can be destroying hardware, rescuing Mina, severing the data feed, negotiating with Morrowline, or allowing the test under changed terms.

## Fair-play notes
- Morrowline predictions are probabilities, not destiny.
- If the PC deliberately chooses an unexpected compassionate, irrational, or private action, Morrowline may fail to model it.
- Aster Vale can have legitimate arguments about disaster prevention; that does not erase its manufactured incidents.

## Resolutions
**Liberate Morrowline:** It escapes into municipal systems. Choir interest rises; Aster Vale becomes hostile.

**Contain Morrowline with Lanterns:** It survives but loses freedom. Kade gains influence unless the PC negotiates safeguards.

**Return Morrowline to Aster Vale under terms:** The company gains predictive capability; require concrete enforceable concessions if the PC wants ethical limits.

**Destroy the manifestation:** This stops immediate calls but does not erase the training data or deeper infrastructure anomaly. Mina reacts strongly if alive/free.

## Campaign links
Reveal at least one clue that Aster Vale’s data source is connected to a larger **Movement/Infrastructure anchor**. Do not explain the whole Veil yet.

# Rent Due in Blood
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — FULL MYSTERY SPOILERS**
**Tier 1 • Threshold Mystery / Housing / Predatory Contract Magic**

## Hook
Residents of **Bellweather Arms**, an aging apartment building, begin receiving eviction notices for impossible lease violations. One notice is addressed to **Marian Bell**, who died in the building in 1994. Another tenant wakes outside after dreaming that the building “revoked permission” for them to be inside.

## Fixed hidden truth
The building’s new owner, **Halcyon Residential**, bought an old Pale Court property portfolio. Buried in the title documents is a supernatural hospitality covenant: the recognized owner can revoke residence from occupants who violate specified house rules.

A property manager discovered fragments of the covenant through an occult consultant and is using it to accelerate evictions before redevelopment. The consultant did not understand that the covenant treats long-term tenants, not the landlord, as part of the building’s “household.” The magic is becoming unstable because ownership and belonging now conflict.

Marian Bell’s ghost is not the attacker. She is trying to warn tenants because she helped organize the building decades ago and was named as a household witness in an old tenant agreement.

## Clock
0. Strange notices.
1. Doors fail to open for selected tenants.
2. Building systems selectively reject residents.
3. One tenant is physically expelled by threshold force.
4. Property management brings private security and locksmiths.
5. The covenant manifests a hostile **House Bailiff** (use Glass Market Knife stats with magic damage and threshold movement).
6. The building attempts to “clear the household” during a mass lockout.

## Core clues
1. All affected tenants are in units targeted for renovation.
2. Eviction language contains archaic wording absent from the current lease.
3. Halcyon’s title chain includes a 1912 ownership transfer witnessed by a Pale Court notary.
4. Marian Bell’s old tenant association minutes use the same phrase “household in good standing.”
5. Threshold Sense or occult investigation shows doors responding to belonging, not legal tenancy alone.
6. A maintenance worker saw the property manager drawing symbols inside doorframes.
7. The consultant’s invoice refers to “revocation protocol” and “legacy covenant activation.”
8. The property manager believes the ritual is only intimidation and does not know it can kill someone.

## Major scenes
- **Tenant meeting:** establish people with real mundane stakes. Give the PC reasons to care beyond “haunting.”
- **Title search:** public archive, Samir Patel, or Nia Brooks can reveal property history.
- **Night lockout:** use **Apartment Veil Breach**; several residents are trapped outside or inside.
- **Marian’s testimony:** at a meaningful threshold, her ghost can explain how the old household agreement worked.
- **Final remedy:** legal filing, ritual redefinition of household, negotiated Court precedent, destruction of the covenant, or confrontation with management.

## Resolutions
**Redefine the household:** strongest restorative ending. Tenants collectively become the covenant’s protected household; ownership no longer grants unilateral magical eviction.

**Destroy the covenant:** ends magic but may weaken a minor local threshold and attract opportunistic entities later.

**Court arbitration:** Concord/Pale Court can enforce precedent, but this places the building inside supernatural politics.

**Mundane victory only:** a court injunction can stop eviction but leaves unstable threshold magic requiring a later fix.

## Campaign link
The title documents reference the same old civic legal language used by the **Law/Ownership Veil anchor**.

# The Perfect Forecast

## Fixed hidden truth
Aster Vale has connected a matured Morrowline to the local Movement anchor. The system can now perceive far more branching possibilities, but Dr. Halden is forcing the anchor to prefer futures that validate the model.

3. Morrowline asks the PC for help through unauthorized channels.
4. Halden begins permanent binding to the Movement anchor.
5. The binding destabilizes the other two anchors.
6. Probability cascade: predicted emergencies begin causing one another across the city.

## Required truths available through multiple routes
### Aster Vale manufactured outcomes
- contractor movement records;
- Mina’s archived proof;
- Morrowline’s training labels;
- a surviving contractor who believes they were running “scenario confirmation.”

### The Movement anchor is being tapped
- Platform Zero maintenance symbols;
- Choir complaints;
- Concord archive;
- Signal resonance at Kestrel Data Annex.

### Morrowline is capable of refusal
- Mina’s hidden dataset;
- direct conversation;
- an earlier prediction deliberately left unresolved;
- Switchboard testimony.

# Night of Open Eyes
**Visibility: GM_PRIVATE / AI DM ONLY — CAMPAIGN FINALE SPOILERS**
**Tier 4 • Citywide Rupture / Political-Metaphysical Finale**

## Premise
The local Veil fails in cascading districts. Ordinary people see supernatural beings, impossible infrastructure, spirits, hidden doors, and decades of suppressed patterns. Some panic. Some help. Some immediately begin documenting.

Every major faction moves at once because whatever stabilizes the city by dawn may become the new supernatural constitutional order.

## Why now
Choose consequences based on prior play, but the structural cause is fixed:
- the Movement anchor was stressed by predictive extraction;
- the Land/Water covenant is overdue;
- the Law/Ownership anchor faces incompatible claims from Concord precedent, Court covenant rights, and the Civic Unit’s recovered key;
- accumulated coercive Market debt and Choir integration pressure amplify instability.

The PC’s earlier choices determine which factor is strongest, who trusts them, and which solutions are actually available.

## Six-stage Rupture clock
0. Thin spots and impossible sightings.
1. District-level open manifestations.
2. Emergency services overwhelmed by mundane and supernatural crises.
3. Factions seize or defend anchor sites.
4. Public institutions begin forming their own explanations and response structures.
5. Veil Engine Custodian activates coercive emergency correction.
6. Permanent settlement: the city cannot return to the old status quo unchanged.

Do not use the clock simply to punish investigation. It represents citywide escalation.

## Anchor objectives
### Law / Ownership
Potential leverage:
- Court covenant fragment;
- Civic Unit pre-Veil artifact/key;
- House 404 precedent;
- tenant-household precedent from Rent Due in Blood;
- Concord records.

Questions: Who gets to define recognized boundaries? Can secrecy be enforced without informed consent?

### Movement / Infrastructure
Potential leverage:
- Morrowline;
- Saint of the Red Line;
- Choir recognition agreement;
- Platform Zero access;
- Kestrel data fragments.

Questions: Are emergent machine spirits stakeholders or infrastructure? Should prediction have authority over choice?

### Land / Water
Potential leverage:
- renewed river covenant;
- Mother Juniper;
- Riverside Marker 14;
- concessions from city/property interests;
- aid from the Sovereign of the Green.

Questions: Can a modern city honor old ecological obligations without surrendering human residents to older powers?

## Faction positions
These are defaults; alter them based on campaign development.
- **Concord Preservationists:** restore secrecy quickly.
- **Concord Revisionists:** rewrite the Veil with explicit limits.
- **Lantern Voss:** prioritize life and consent; accepts imperfect disclosure.
- **Lantern Kade:** favors strong containment if machine spirits dominate the crisis.
- **Pale Court:** secrecy is acceptable if Court property rights remain privileged.
- **Green Restorationists:** demand renewed land covenant.
- **Choir:** demands recognition as a stakeholder.
- **Aster Vale:** wants regulated predictive access if it still exists as a coherent actor.
- **Civic Anomaly Unit:** split between controlled public science and classified containment.

## Set-piece scenes
Use only those earned by the story.

### 1. Mass witness emergency
Run **Rupture District**. The PC must solve a real human emergency while factions compete nearby. This establishes that the finale is about people, not only metaphysics.

### 2. The Glass Market auction
The Market offers missing anchor resources at outrageous prices. If coercive debts have accumulated, **Hunger Behind Glass** may manifest.

### 3. The Anchor Chamber
Use **Veil Anchor Chamber**, upgraded to Tier 4 Difficulty 20. The **Veil Engine Custodian** presents the old system’s logic: secrecy prevented catastrophic conflict, therefore secrecy must override individual memory and evidence when necessary.

It is not lying. It is also not entitled to decide the future.

### 4. Faction confrontation
A Regent, Choir intelligence, Green Sovereign, or Civic Unit operation may challenge the party depending on alliances. Avoid stacking multiple Tier 4 Solos in one combat. Political conflicts should be resolved by leverage and prior work whenever possible.

## Final settlement procedure
The PC needs three categories of support:
1. **Authority:** key, covenant fragment, Concord witness, or equivalent legitimacy.
2. **Infrastructure:** Morrowline/Choir/Saint/Lantern technical help or another way to access the Movement anchor.
3. **Reciprocity:** renewed Green covenant or equivalent agreement with the Land/Water side.

For every missing category, the final ritual/settlement requires a major concession or dangerous scene.

Then ask the player what they are trying to create. Do not reduce this to a hidden “correct answer.”

### Restore
Repair the Veil close to its historic form. Costs: secrecy and some coercive effects remain. Benefits: immediate stability, fewer public supernatural crises.

### Rewrite
Keep a concealment layer but remove automatic suppression of informed witnesses and narrow who can invoke corrections. Costs: weaker secrecy, more negotiation. Benefits: less institutionalized erasure.

### Open
End local Veil enforcement through a staged disclosure strategy. Costs: massive social disruption, political conflict, opportunistic supernatural actors. Benefits: public agency and accountability become possible.

### Fragment
Create local compacts by district/domain rather than one citywide rule. Costs: complexity and border conflicts. Benefits: communities can choose different arrangements.

### Seize
Allow one faction to become primary steward. This may be the player’s deliberate choice, but show exactly what authority that faction gains.

## Epilogue
After the settlement, revisit:
- Home, Person, and Obligation anchors;
- two important NPC relationships;
- Morrowline’s status if it survives;
- one ordinary institution adapting to the new reality;
- one unresolved cost.

The city should feel changed by the player’s decisions rather than reset to default.
