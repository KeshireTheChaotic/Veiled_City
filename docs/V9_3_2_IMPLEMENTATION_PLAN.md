## Binding decision / rule precedence

1. Preserve the previously approved **consented, story-first, no-standard-PC-vs-PC-damage** narrative PvP. Two independently owned characters approve identical signed stakes, boundaries, mode and audience. Win by higher *final opposed total* (duel) or first to **three** higher-total paired exchanges; ties award no strike. Natural double retains the `Critical` label but does not trump higher total.
2. **NEW FINAL HOUSE RULE:** **Zero Hope generated by PvP** (Hope result, Critical, both duel and all three-strike exchanges, roller and opponent). `Fear` => **one immediate, meaningful, native-validated GM move per qualifying roll**, resolved in the same exchange, not campaign Fear, banked currency, or deferred spotlight. This fully supersedes all older `hope_policy`/`fear_policy` terms and tests. Normal **non-PvP** Daggerheart Hope/Fear remains unchanged. Full contract: `V9_3_2_PVP_HOPE_FEAR_CONTRACT.md`.
3. **Mandatory unchanged P0:** claim/scene/affected-character/recipient-based output security. A GM result triggered by Billy cannot invent Choso's reaction. Winner success and loser reaction each need current owner source/ratification; GM review cannot substitute for consent. Raw AI prose and ambiguous facts stay quarantined. Mixed valid native actions progress. Normal observable PC appearance/history and NPC/environment descriptions **must still work** with real evidence; missing PC description returns a useful nonblocking unknown. `REFERENCE_V9_3_OUTPUT_AUTHORITY_CONTRACT.md`, `REFERENCE_V9_3_1_OBSERVATION_CONTRACT.md`, and `REFERENCE_V9_2_AUTHORSHIP_CONTRACT.md` remain operative.
4. Normal Daggerheart/SRD: ordinary action roll with Hope/Critical usually grants Hope, Fear ordinarily generates spendable GM Fear, GM moves broadly respond to adverse consequences, and adversaries can spotlight/act through the usual encounter rules. **Our PvP overrides the resource generation and timing only for the custom mode.** Do not apply this restriction to reaction or ordinary SRD paths. Official references: https://daggerheartsrd.com/rules/making-moves-and-taking-action/ and https://daggerheartsrd.com/rules/core-gm-mechanics/ .
