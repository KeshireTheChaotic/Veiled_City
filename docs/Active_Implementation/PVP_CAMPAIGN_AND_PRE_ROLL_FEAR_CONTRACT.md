# Campaign PvP and pre-roll Fear: owner decisions

Owner-authorized 2026-10-19. This is a contract, **not implementation or release qualification**. It supplements the semantic-first plan and does not change historical versioned receipts.

Accepted typed intents are canonical adjudication inputs; human declarations own intent; native receipts own outcomes.

## Campaign conflict mode

- An authenticated GM explicitly enables the new mode; it is disabled by default. Enablement is not a player signature, character decision, roll, damage receipt or disclosure.
- Legitimate source-bound PC conflict need not repeat a bilateral approval ceremony. The new version must verify the actual current actor, target, scene, source, principal, reach and native rules at execution, not merely preparation.
- Any affected owner can immediately safety-stop their character's participation. It takes priority over a pending hostile-effect commitment, including an AI action being processed. It cannot wait for narration or a model request. Silence, absence, inactivity and in-fiction words never substitute for voluntary authorization.
- A stop prevents new/unfinished hostile resolution, not independent players' actions. It never reverses committed dice, costs, damage or outcomes. Repeating a saved operation may read its native result without applying it again.
- Armor, optional reactions, voluntary resource choices and Daggerheart death moves remain with the rightful owner. No automatic death choice, extra defender roll, compelled reaction or information disclosure.
- Saved bilateral agreements, pending records and historical attack/damage/death readers remain readable and recoverable under their original versions. No fabricated signatures or rewritten historical terms.
- The first bounded safety-control adapter may keep a stop sticky. A later resume/re-enrollment must require an explicit current owner decision and fresh native scope; it cannot revive old hostile work silently. A GM cannot override the owner's stop.
- Narration cannot bypass the stop or establish an uncommitted mechanical effect. Expressive attempts/threats remain distinct from completed effects.

## Pre-roll Fear interruption

- Exactly **1 already available campaign Fear** buys one otherwise-legal, source-validated native NPC spotlight before an affected player's **unrolled** action. It grants no automatic success, extra roll modifier, additional NPC action or control of a PC choice.
- A pending PC roll cannot generate Fear to fund its own interruption. Existing post-action Fear obligations and this campaign-pool expenditure are distinct causal accounts; neither consumes or charges the other.
- Announce the exact spend and intended intervention before the PC rolls. Require actual acknowledged delivery of that exact native announcement. A prepared message, transport invocation, model claim, failed send or uncertain send is not acknowledgement.
- Printed feature costs are additional and independently validated. A single idempotent transaction must not charge the same spotlight or printed cost twice through two gateways.
- The pending PC roll stays held until the announcement and native interruption reach a lawful terminal state or safe cancellation. Independent actions remain available. Previously committed rolls/outcomes cannot be modified.
- Reservation, acknowledgement, commitment, cancellation and restart require durable native ancestry. Concurrent campaign spends cannot consume reserved Fear; cancellation cannot create Fear or silently lose a held reservation at the pool cap.
- NPC attacks still require legitimate encounter/template/presence, actual native dice and rightful defender/Armor/death choices. A purchased spotlight does not waive those requirements.

## Qualification boundary

Both decisions unblock implementation only. Keep the replacement inert until its current producer, pre-effect guards, saved readers, private output, deferred delivery, safety priority, concurrency and restart tests qualify. Existing non-PvP SRD behavior and post-action PvP Fear remain intact. No production access, live Discord, paid calls or deployment is authorized by these decisions.
