## Draft authority boundaries to formalize in V96-00

Accepted typed intents are canonical adjudication inputs; human declarations own intent; native receipts own outcomes.

- **Human owner:** exact original declaration, own choices, optional spends, consent, own reaction/withdrawal/disclosure. A proposed alternate tactic remains distinct and unaccepted until the owner chooses it.
- **AI GM:** ordinary fictional understanding, contextual response and candidate trait/Difficulty/stakes using bounded existing rules. Cannot grant a capability, forge a principal/reference/receipt or adjudicate another player's assent.
- **Native validator:** current owner/source/occurrence/scene/target/audience/ruling/feature eligibility and version checks. A generic standard roll never substitutes for missing special-feature permission or a dedicated effect adapter.
- **Native dice/effect services:** exactly-once saved outcomes/costs/material effects. A no-roll result uses legitimate observable facts and zero-cost native authority; a standard uncertain roll has no invented success before dice.
- **Human GM review:** scoped authenticated rules/capability ambiguity or unsupported adapter resolution, not automatic takeover of another PC's intent/consent/reaction. Review cannot rewrite committed results or waive unresolved Fear.
- **Publication/retrieval:** receipt/owner/audience revalidation at immediate and deferred sinks, summaries, journals, shared memory and voice. Never authorize effects from a model claim list, a lexical cue or narration.

Keep PvP distinct: no Hope or action-Critical Stress award; each qualifying action Fear creates its immediate native obligation; defender reactions do not create extra moves. Narrative modes do not apply HP damage; damage-enabled attacks retain target-specific signed terms, Evasion, owner defense/Armor and vanilla death rules. Existing human/NPC-proxy control stays intact.

## Planning risks / required phase decisions

- **Generic-rule overreach:** prove a real local SRD generic-action reference and current v10/saved-ruling applicability. Do not turn absence of a bespoke verb handler into review, but do hold actual capability/rule/adapter absence. Separate these reasons in status and tests.
- **Lexical retirement:** inventory every reachable branch's authority role first. Exact syntax/source spans/identity checks and read-only ranking are not keyword gameplay authority. Suspicious-prose tripwires may hold for review as defense-in-depth, never mint intent or native effects; require idiom positives so they do not silently reintroduce broad false rejection.
- **Optimization prerequisites:** do not optimize ahead of V96-01–05 behavior stability. Preserve expressive NPC/GM fiction, salient lore, conflicting evidence and response duty. No automatic model switch or weakened narrative model.
- **Flags/readers:** new discretion remains opt-in until qualified; proposed record/version names are not implemented. Decide exact schema, feature flag and compatibility behavior from actual source during the owning ticket. Keep historical records read-only, unfamiliar effects held and rollback non-destructive.
- **Package versus app version:** v9.6 names this work cycle; application remains 10.0.0 unless a separate version instruction is given. No automatic version bump is planned here.
