## Authority and delivery

Accepted typed intents are canonical adjudication inputs; human declarations own intent; native receipts own outcomes. GM narration projects authorized fiction. Ordinary supported AI-GM rulings do not need a fabricated human approval. The GM cannot execute a player's Duality Dice or choose their Armor, consent, reaction, surrender or death move.

GM-origin NPC proposals have native immutable events independent of owner-authored spans. Current source, actor, owner/control, scene, native target, rules, opportunity and costs are checked before execution and again for delivery. First normal GM spotlight is not universally charged Fear. Additional spotlight and allowed native Experience expenses are additive before RNG. Ordinary nonattack NPC behavior is not automatically rolled. GM d20 never generates Hope/Fear; PC reactions never generate a GM move or metacurrency merely by rolling. Protected PvP remains zero-Hope with its existing immediate Fear pipeline.

Actual queued/delivered positive text includes `Rain beads on the window.`, `You feel warm air from the dryers.`, and attributed `Have you seen Raven?`. They are native constructions, not raw-text exceptions. Original `Choso apologizes for losing.`, `Mr. Raven, you agreed to surrender.`, unverified PC injury/choice/custody, and private disclosure negatives remain mandatory. Exact destination arrival and interior description, contextual inside query, independent signs and per-NPC task coverage remain in the audience suite.

Private pending terms contain native-owned trait/Difficulty/request identity and bounded attempt-only stakes, never arbitrary GM proposal rationale. Real fake transport confirms delivery once; restart/source retraction confirms zero stale sends. Native roll/damage/effect proof remains authoritative if narration is held. Private prerequisites do not become NPC knowledge/public effect authorization; they stay reviewed unless separate disclosure/observable proof exists.
