# PvP examples — consent, authorship and risk

Illustrative text only, not actual dice, consent, receipts or a promise of unrestricted AI narration. Public views below require current agreed audience and explicit owner disclosure. Private traits, HP, Armor choices and sealed dice do not automatically appear in a party channel. Accepted typed intents are canonical adjudication inputs; human declarations own intent; native receipts own outcomes.

## Declined or withdrawn

**Jaxon:** “I attempt a harmless shin tap.”

**Private offer to Choso:** Review the exact proposed stakes, boundaries, mode and audience. Accept, counteroffer or decline; no roll or PC action has been authorized yet.

**Choso privately declines.**

**Permitted neutral status:** “This contest is closed without resolution. No character response or forfeiture is implied.” No dice, injury, Hope gain, compelled apology or punishment follows. Withdrawal stops future hostile actions but does not erase a previously committed native result or genuine immediate Fear obligation.

## Harmless narrative: single-roll duel

Both owners accept the same current terms: harmless tap only; no HP/Armor/Stress change, forced speech or reaction; agreed publication audience. Each privately selects and authorizes their own native roll.

**After both saved rolls and authorized disclosure:** Jaxon total 17, Choso total 13. The higher total wins the agreed stake; a lower-total natural Critical would not override it.

**Verified stake view:** “Jaxon wins the agreed harmless-tap stake.”

**Jaxon's independently source-verified own clause:** “I land my own harmless tap.”

**Choso withholds his reaction.** The system adds none. Jaxon submitting “Choso apologizes for losing.” is quarantined privately, not published as fact. A separate current Choso-authored/ratified permitted clause and separate disclosure approval are required for Choso's response. No Hope or Critical Stress clear is generated.

## Harmless narrative: Three Strikes

Both owners separately approve Three Strikes under the same no-harm boundaries. Five native paired exchanges award strikes Jaxon, Choso, Jaxon, Choso, Jaxon: **3–2**. Tied totals award no strike; silence never forfeits an exchange. The saved winner does not authorize a loser reaction or injury. Owners may submit/withhold their own bounded clauses as above.

If an exchange contains one Fear roll, one meaningful source-grounded GM consequence is due; two Fear rolls require two distinct ordered consequences. A real reviewed countdown may advance once per distinct effect. No cosmetic `lights_flicker`, future spotlight credit, campaign Fear banking, Hope award or invented PC response settles an obligation. Pending consequences hold dependent continuation, not independent Billy/Tyrell actions.

## Damage-enabled: vanilla damage/death risk

This is a separate v3 consent profile, not an extra effect of winning a duel/strike. Every affected owner approves actual target edges, risk/death disclosure, stop rules, permitted features and audience. Native attack review uses the actual target's current Evasion. The defender gets no automatic extra opposed roll; a separately sourced applicable reaction has its own owner choice.

**Private native workflow example:** saved attack total 15 versus reviewed Evasion 12 → hit; saved damage 10 versus reviewed Major 7/Severe 14 → two HP marks before Armor. The target independently chooses applicable Armor, reducing severity by one to one HP mark. These are illustrative values, never values inferred or supplied by narration. Only the native damage/Armor effect commits the actual result, once.

**Bounded party status, only if separately authorized:** “The native attack has a verified outcome. The defender's reaction remains theirs to describe.” Confidential numbers and choices remain private unless their owner approves exact disclosure. Attacker action Fear creates its own immediate obligation; an independently source-bound NPC Fear attack uses separate native Evasion/defense/damage/Armor receipts, never a generic HP event. No PvP Hope or action-Critical Stress clear occurs.

At actual final HP, the affected owner privately selects the native death move. GM or attacker cannot choose it. Separately selected Risk It All retains vanilla matching-death-dice full HP/Stress recovery; this is not the disabled PvP action-Critical bonus. No death, scar prose or resurrection is invented by a party summary.

## Guaranteed-nonlethal damage: unavailable

The owner chose vanilla damage/death risk and disabled both guaranteed-nonlethal damage tiers. A proposed last-HP cap or nonlethal fall is rejected before damage consent/rolls. Use harmless narrative PvP, or negotiate the separately disclosed vanilla-risk damage profile. The bot does not quietly cap HP, bypass a death choice or claim guaranteed safety.

## Three-plus PCs / teams

Each current owner approves the same group revision and their exact permitted target edges. In a three-PC free-for-all, independently accepted opportunities are elective in any fair order; one attacker cannot repeatedly monopolize them. In 2v2, signed opposing edges do not imply friendly fire. Independently authorized simultaneous hits on one target aggregate through their native source group before that target's Armor decision; no duplicate hit or hidden attacker is inferred.

An uninvolved Billy can ask about Jaxon's appearance while the clash is pending. The reply reports only observer-visible established facts, or truthfully says that no description has been established; it does not fail or demand a description. Tyrell can receive the established visible Station Attendant's blue-coat description. Neither question authorizes a new PC appearance, apology, secret disclosure, participation or damage.
