## Native source / task sidecar

Any task ledger is a separate native, versioned record, not an extra property inserted into closed v8 rows. Stable identity derives from actual owner/source hash, source clause start/end, operation, canonical target, session/scene, principal revision and effective audience. It supplies no effect, consent, knowledge or authoring permission. Current source offsets and target eligibility must be rechecked; unsupported/ambiguous mappings remain held or not_attempted. A model label cannot certify its own answer or prose safety.

Per-task dispositions: answered, legitimately_unavailable, needs_clarification, held, not_attempted. Category-level existence is not aggregate completion. Distinct targets require distinct validated contributions. Same-target ambiguous mapping cannot be silently duplicated. Delivery acknowledgment and generation/projection coverage are distinct stages; unacknowledged output never grants memory.
