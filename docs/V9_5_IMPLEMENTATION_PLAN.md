# Veiled City — V9.5 implementation and safe-deprecation plan

**Status:** PROPOSED / NOT IMPLEMENTED. **Source reviewed:** `KeshireTheChaotic/Veiled_City`, branch `codex/typed-intent-refinement`, HEAD `e88a4414401b719bf17826b568fcfefac5eeabd4` (2026-10-12). **Base:** v9.4/V9-08 offline-qualified, not live-qualified. **Do not rewrite v9.4 history or re-open passed P0 issues without new evidence.**

## 0. Mission, boundaries, authority

Preserve the story-first, natural-language AI GM and the v9.4 independent native adjudication system while addressing the actual live-play gaps: (i) ordinary NPC and world prose suppressed in protected PvP scenes, (ii) Fear needing manual GM selection or a preexisting eligible target, (iii) high-friction JSON-only PvP control path, (iv) lack of real-player/model latency and quality evidence, and (v) accumulated legacy/dead tests and source. Keep player agency, Daggerheart rules and non-PvP mechanics unchanged unless a separate, explicitly approved house rule applies. No blanket raw-LLM trust, safety-check bypass, auto-consent, unauthorized resource effects, altered saved dice, or new per-roll PvP Hope.

**Grounded baseline:** Release HEAD has a successful `offline-validation` GitHub Actions workflow; progress documents record **126/126 mandatory offline suites and 473/473 focused tests** for V9-08. These are recorded historical qualifications, not new test runs by this plan. D08 is **PASS for offline qualification**; v9.5/live release begins **UNQUALIFIED** until its own gates pass. The user explicitly prohibits commits/pushes from chat. **All subsequent code changes, commits, and any authorized fast-forward push belong to Codex**, with a phase gate. Production pull/deployment/Discord registration/billable API work need separate approval.

**Invariants, mandatory for every phase:**

1. Authenticated player source determines intent; independently authorized native receipts determine dice, effects, HP/Stress/Armor/Hope/Fear/death and movement. Model-proposed claims are not completeness or authority evidence.
2. Other PCs' speech, emotions, choices, voluntary responses and private details cannot be authored by GM/model or another player. Every output sink (Discord party/private, director, voice, journal, summary, memory, history, replay) enforces exact claim/scene/affected-owner/audience proof. Protected narrative is not a reason to silence valid independent NPC/world action.
3. Narrative PvP: bilateral opposed-total / first to 3; no damage from strike. Damage PvP: signed v3, per-target consent, target Evasion and native damage/Armor/HP/death path. v9.4 currently permits **vanilla lethal-risk damage only**, disables guaranteed-nonlethal profiles and disables PvP action-Critical Stress clearance (owner rulings); do not silently reverse. Multi-PC damage FFA/teams use native spotlights/locks; no invented N-person Three Strikes.
4. PvP rolls generate **zero Hope**, including Critical; qualifying PvP **action** Fear requires immediate independently verified consequential GM move, never campaign Fear or future enemy spotlight. Normal defender reaction rolls generate neither Hope nor Fear moves. Both qualifying Fear rolls have separate idempotent obligations. Existing non-PvP Hope/Fear and valid death moves remain unaffected.
5. Current v9.4's strict idempotency, consent revisions, source privacy, evidence and recovery are non-negotiable. Dirty/untracked local files, imported characters, `.env`, SQLite/WAL/SHM and previously delivered/uncertain outbox messages are preserved.

### V95-01 — make ordinary GM narration playable in protected scenes

**Verified seams:** `bot/src/pvp-output.js` `protectedAgreements()`/`protectedPvpScope()` (currently enumerate all same-scene bilateral and v3 contracts), `prepareAuthorizedTurnOutput()` (drops raw prose, emits observation, native action, Fear and owner clauses or a generic neutral), `renderPart()`/`validateOutputPart()`/`revalidateOutputPublication()`; `bot/src/world-observation.js` and `character-observation.js` currently validate selected known facts, not spontaneous fresh NPC speech. GM source `bot/src/gm.js`, mutation pipeline `state.js`, `turn-orchestration.js`, `index.js`/private and World Director, journals/voice/recaps.

1. Separate **historical authorship hazard** from **active mechanical PvP progression**. Completed/declined/withdrawn proposals may still require protection from retrospective invented PC reactions, but must **not** automatically downgrade unrelated scene dialogue to neutral. Do not simply filter archived records out of the authority check or release raw text unconditionally.
2. Define closed, versioned GM-owned output parts, e.g. `npc_dialogue`, `npc_action_observation`, `scene_atmosphere`, `world_situation`, `observation_answer`, `native_consequence`, `owner_clause`, with `source_ref`, `scene/session`, `entity/observer`, audience, `world_authority_ref`, data model hash/revision, and deterministic renderer/template version. A natural-language GM proposal can be creative, but **cannot certify itself** as valid character ownership, mechanical effects or private disclosure. An explicit server-side source-binding/eligibility step owns each publishable part. For novel dialogue, establish a native NPC utterance (speaker is existing/legitimately native-created GM-owned NPC, current location and voice scope, no unauthorized PC quoted speech or invented knowledge) separately from PC action/effect. Novel atmospheric prose may be first established in an appropriately bounded world-authoring source; material access, threats, items, injuries and lore still need native outcomes/canon permission.
3. Content safety: arbitrary prose cannot be proven semantically safe via `narrative_claims:[]`, `safe:true`, regex or a second LLM alone. Use a **closed output projection** constructed from individually verified native parts, with exact source spans/render data. Any unprojectable/ambiguous material claim is quarantined privately, not published. If rich free text is desired, require an explicit GM-authored review/ratification pathway that does not override PC ownership. Never publish an unverified full paragraph because its NPC speaker was valid.
4. Preserve useful PC appearance questions (unknown description = truthful nonblocking answer), GM-controlled NPC spontaneous conversation, observable environment, third-person harmless prose, active PC consent/ownership and independent Billy's movement in the same turn. Do not require every mundane color detail to be preexisting; GM may establish its own nonmechanical fiction under an audited scope.
5. At **every** sink—native outbox, journal/recap, TTS, private, director and replay—rerender/revalidate from saved verified parts. Existing successfully delivered historical outputs remain immutable history; don't scrape raw quarantines into summaries. Prevent replay from duplicating NPC dialogue as a new fact or from cross-audience exposure.
6. Write red-first **NAR01–NAR18** tests including resolved, declined, withdrawn PvP same scene; old opponent remains scene present; bartender speaks naturally with full meaningful response; newly introduced NPC via authorized world creation; unknown/partial PC description; contrast invented PC apology, secrets, material effects, contradictory NPC facts, delivery at different audience after restart; Billy independent action/Choso unwilling response.

**Acceptance:** protected PvP narration is neither the neutral fallback for ordinary grounded GM scenes nor a raw AI escape hatch. Baseline PVP30/PVP30q, FH40, OBS21 and all journal/privacy/memory regression assertions unchanged. Quality fixture measures communicative NPC response, not only presence of keyword or nonempty text.

3. **No-context scenes:** permit native, scoped creation of a proportionate **new** complication via GM world-authoring (e.g. a fresh deadline/clock with agreed fiction, a new authorized NPC entrance/threat, a newly blocked route) *before* applying its first effect. 5. Two-Fear paired narrative rolls resolve as two independent ordered obligations; two independent PvP attackers each create one if qualifying. A single fictional beat can contain bounded linked subeffects only with distinct valid obligations/effect receipts, never silently coalesce two into one or charge for three. 7. **FEA01–FEA20** tests: no known clock/NPC, contextual move materializes then effects once; two-Fear, tie, win/loss, three strikes, 4-PC brawl, immediate NPC interruption, expired consent, owner-target privacy, adversary attack full native receipts, existing/fresh context, unsupported mapping, bounded timeout, crash/restart and non-PvP economy.
