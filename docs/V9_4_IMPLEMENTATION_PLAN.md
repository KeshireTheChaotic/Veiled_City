## Sources and rule precedence

1. **Official SRD 2.0:** before dice, agree on a PC conflict's terms; rolls against PCs use **Evasion**; PC damage uses damage dice, Armor, thresholds, marked HP and selected death moves; a reaction roll creates no Hope, Fear, GM move or Critical Hope/Stress bonus. Ordinary critical attack automatically succeeds and increases rolled damage. See `V9_4_RULES_AND_COMMUNITY_RESEARCH.md`.
2. **Veiled City existing house rules:** signed consent and author-owned narration, Duel higher final opposed total (Critical is descriptive and **does not beat a higher total**), Three Strikes first to 3 verified paired wins. No combat damage from these narrative scores. PvP action rolls **generate zero Hope**; qualifying action-roll Fear yields an **immediate consequential** GM move, never campaign Fear. These override normal resource generation, but **not** ordinary non-PvP actions.
3. **New Veiled City profile:** `damage_enabled_pvp`, separately and explicitly consented, uses PC attack-vs-current-target-Evasion by default; appropriate reaction rolls only when a rule/hazard or explicit contested-action protocol calls for them. It uses actual attack/damage/Armor/HP with capped consent/stop/death handling. Damage is never inferred from a narrative win. This is a **new house-rule authorization framework** using otherwise official combat procedures—not a published Daggerheart PvP combat subsystem.
4. **Table/community suggestions:** equal opportunities, short exchanges, stakes/countdown alternatives, nonlethal boundaries, group spotlight fairness and keeping player disagreement out of character conflict are informative, not automatically binding. **No blanket +2/+3 Evasion, no free reaction against every PC attack, no opponent-spendable Fear pool, no automatic bonus Hope, no third-party commercial homebrew import.** See research decision table.
5. **Authorization is claim/scene/affected character/audience/effect**, never merely triggering player or label. GM outputs cannot directly create PC HP/Stress/Armor/Hope deltas; raw AI text never substitutes for source-linked native effects. Good NPC/scene descriptions and ordinary independent acts remain playable.

## Core native contracts (additive, not in-place data rewrites)

### Profile and consent

`pvp_encounter_contract` v3: `{id, guild, session, scene, profile: narrative_only|damage_enabled, participants:[{character,owner,principal_revision,team?}], interaction_scope: duel|free_for_all|teams, objective, target_edges:[{attacker,target,permitted_effect_types}], stakes, max_severity, lethality: harmless|nonlethal_no_incapacitation|nonlethal_incapacitation|lethal, last_hp_policy, stop_rules, max_exchanges?, joined_at_revision, authorized_roll_types, permitted_features, option_spending, spotlight_policy, combined_scoring_policy?, audience, terms_hash, source_event, revision}`. This is a proposed schema, not proof of an implemented DB table. Strict versioning, closed fields, digest and live owner/scene/acceptance check. Full encounter terms explicitly presented to all affected owners; **each target must separately authorize their own risk tier and every targeted action must be allowed by the currently signed edge**. One actor owning two PCs does not count as two independent players unless table policy explicitly allows multi-PC control and independent affected-character approvals; never infer missing signatures. New group member or elevated lethality invalidates affected approvals and cannot apply retroactively after rolls.

Narrative-only stays on the current two-party, version-2 consent/round service. Keep `no_HP_armor_stress_damage`. New multi-PC narrative objectives require a separate agreed group scoring policy; do **not** turn a bilateral 3-strikes algorithm into an N-person automatic winner. Damage-enabled uses native PC attack; a `pvp_strike_receipt`/duel total can **never** serve as damage authorization. If users elect **both** score and damage, require `combined_scoring_policy={objective, scoring_event, native_hit_required:true, damage_independent:true, completion_order, tie_policy, stop_rule}` before any roll; never infer damage from a strike, and do not count a missed attack as damaging because it won a story beat.

### Risk tiers and death

- `harmless`: narrative_only; no HP, Stress, Armor or death from direct opponent attack.
- `nonlethal_no_incapacitation`: damage-enabled only with an **explicitly signed** house-rule stop **before last HP is marked** (cap incoming marked HP to leave one unmarked slot, record overflow stopped as `pvp_safety_stop_receipt`, close the attack/contest without converting an attack hit to failure). This is *not* ordinary SRD damage processing, and must be presented as such; if players prefer a different cap, obtain a separate table-approved profile and tests before implementation.
- `nonlethal_incapacitation`: may mark last HP; substitute an **agreed nonlethal fall/incapacitated status**, never invoke `Blaze of Glory`, `Avoid Death` or `Risk It All` automatically. Define clearing/recovery and defender control over reactions before terms lock. The override is a Veiled City house rule.
- `lethal`: may mark last HP and enter the **actual selected, PC-owned SRD death-move workflow**. Lethal consent must name death-move/irreversible risks, permanence and stopping conditions, be separately authenticated by affected PCs, and be revocable until committed attack effects. GM cannot choose death move or roleplay permanent character loss for the PC. Forced death without a selected move is forbidden except a separately approved scenario explicitly within the SRD's exceptional-fiction warning, which should NOT be automated in v9.4.

Damage permission alone authorizes **none** of forced speech, secrets, confiscation, social confession, control of PC, lasting scar or later lethal escalation. Stop/withdrawal is neutral: stop future hostile actions, preserve valid committed damage/effects, no retroactive reroll, unapproved revenge or forfeit. An injury is not the attacker's license to author the defender's emotions.

### Attack/reaction/damage/source receipt chain

`pvp_attack_intent` -> `pvp_target_consent_proof` -> `pvp_attack_roll_receipt` (actual Hope/Fear d12 faces, trait, mods, current target Evasion, natural critical) -> optional `pvp_defense_option_receipt` / `pvp_reaction_roll_receipt` only if separately authorized by an applicable feature/hazard/opposed maneuver -> `pvp_damage_roll_receipt` (actual weapon/spell/feature source, proficiency/parsed dice/damage type, roll dice and crit max dice addition) -> `pvp_armor_decision_receipt` (target owner, slot availability, feature, applicable reduction) -> `pc_effect_receipt` (exact target, before/after HP/Armor/Stress/conditions, source ids, damage marks, signed lethality stop) -> `pvp_action_terminal_receipt`. Distinguish target-specific outcomes; avoid a single party-scope `resource_delta`. Missing equipment/trait/range/LOS, feature mapping, current Evasion, defense ownership, last-HP choice or resource authority -> explicit pending ruling, no applied effect.

Native damage order: valid hit (attack vs **current target Evasion**, natural Critical on *attack* auto-success), roll typed damage using proficiency dice, apply allowed critical max *dice* bonus, resistance/immunity and direct-damage handling, compare to current Major/Severe thresholds, permit defendant's own Armor Slot decision, then mark **0–3 HP** as rules require and apply the explicitly signed last-HP policy. Effects of actual simultaneous multiple sources must follow SRD aggregation before threshold application, with exact source-group identity and each contributor consumed once. Clarify that Armor reduces **severity by one threshold per slot** normally; armor is not a flat subtraction from the roll. Verify equipment/Armor features and conditions at time of use.

**Defensive reaction caveat:** SRD does not require a separate opposed defender roll for every attack. Standard PC attack uses the target's **Evasion**. Features, hazards or agreed nonattack opposed maneuvers may call for a separately owner-authorized **reaction roll**. Such reactions do not gain Hope/Fear, trigger extra GM moves, clear Stress on critical, or award critical attack damage. An actual critical reaction avoids the effects a normal successful reaction would still suffer, where the source effect provides that distinction. Never give an automatic attack-blocking reaction by attaching a `pvp_reaction` label to an ordinary attack.

**Critical house-rule reconciliation:** narrative duel/strikes: matching natural dice record Critical but cannot outrank greater opposed total; zero Hope; no generic Critical Stress clear. Damage-enabled PC **action/attack**: natural Critical auto-hits and adds max possible **damage dice** per SRD, while still **generating zero PvP Hope**. Propose retaining the ordinary SRD critical Stress-clear (not Hope), *unless group consent expressly disables that bonus*, and make the selected `critical_stress_policy` fixed before rolling. It is an explicit Veiled City house-rule decision to separate these critical bonuses; the owner can approve/alter before implementation, never ad hoc. In either event, prevent critical-generated Hope through generic award code. Non-PvP crits unchanged.

### Source-bound PC effect gate (FH40: mandatory first)

`pc_effect_authority` v1: `{effect_id, resource_or_condition, target_character_id, proposed_delta, cause_type: verified_damage|verified_feature_cost|verified_healing|verified_npc_attack|verified_environment_effect|verified_consequence, cause_receipt, source_event, campaign, session, scene, principal_revision_or_gm_role, owner_decision_ref?, expected_target_revision, before_digest, target_effect_scope, idempotency_key}`. **No generically accepted AI `resource_delta` is sufficient.** Resolve a recognized native cause and its *permitted* deltas; validate use-time source/scene/target/current status/consent and recorded resource revision; CAS apply exactly once and write effect receipt in same DB transaction. An unsupported generated event yields a blocked `needs_native_cause` diagnostic and does not mutate the target. Preserve independent typed actions, native outcomes, safe NPC narration and existing non-PvP heal/NPC/hazard operations through their own real receipt source. Do not solve FH40 by rejecting every event globally or by fabricating a synthetic damage receipt from GM prose.

### Fear, timing, spotlight and publication

- **PvP action rolls:** zero Hope on Hope/Critical. Qualifying Fear creates exactly one immediate trigger in `pvp_fear_gate`; no `changeFear()` nor enemy-spotlight bank. Narrative paired *action* rolls may create 0, 1 or 2 triggers; every trigger resolved before another paired exchange/dependent scene progression. **Damage-enabled:** each *attacker action* roll with Fear creates one immediate GM move even when the hit succeeds; a defender's ordinary *reaction* is not a new trigger, regardless of Fear die. Two independent simultaneous attacking PCs may each generate a trigger. One per qualifying native action result, not per enemy hit or damage die.
- GM move may be a native NPC attack, threat countdown, interrupted third-party action, established revelation, world/access change, hazard or valid resource effect, proportionate to fiction. For PC damage require independently validated **incoming NPC PC damage** or other legitimate cause, not generic GM `resource_delta`. For third-party interruption, hold unfinished node and preserve already completed work; don't invent loss, cost or response.
- `pvp_fear_trigger` -> `pvp_fear_move_decision` -> `pvp_fear_effect_receipt`, keyed by exact roll and scene. Select/current snapshot/GM authority; commit only supported effects; pending/needs_review blocks affected dependent progression but leaves saved attack/score valid. Multiple triggers have stable ordering; cannot coalesce away one nor double-apply; an effect can be proportionately bounded and share fictional context only when each receipt is distinct and justified.
- Brawl spotlight: advisory fairness ledger independent of roll success/Fear; eligible consenting PC/team should have a meaningful opportunity before an actor receives another **elective** spotlight, except explicit reactions/GM moves/urgent threats. No forced initiative or rigid round; a recovery/withdrawal may relinquish opportunity; an active player cannot monopolize by repeated Hope. GM intervention can be immediate but not secretly grant an attacker extra consecutive attacks. Nonparticipants retain their own parallel scene actions. Spotlight choice is native, auditable, safe from actor/AI forgery, and does not provide damage or Fear spending authority.
- Publish only verified and audience-allowed source clauses, attack/HP statuses and GM effect narration through persisted output parts/outbox, revalidate before enqueue/send/replay/journal/voice. Hide sealed moves, private statistics, roll values, secret facts and unwilling PC reactions. Existing NPC/scenery observations must still work. If a character hasn't described their appearance, answer gracefully and continue. Changing audience doesn't invent consent.
